Use exercises to control the muscles of your eyes, by rolling them open and tightly closed, by striwing to see as far balk of you as you can, and also up and down without moving your head. Roll your eyes in different geometrical figures. Learn to converge them and cross them. Looking at „Urna" point beetween the eyebrows, and bringing them crossed to the tip of the nose. Work to be able to desribe geometrical figures with your eyes crossed. Develop faculty of looking with one eye up and with another down.
