**Breath is life.**

Flowing thru the nostrils, breath shades itself three ways.

When coming thru the right nostril it is creative electric breath and it is feeding the vasomotor system. So called Pingala. — Name of this breath is **Sun Breath** {breath of warrior ready to fight}.

Coming thru the left nostril, it is regulating and mothering principle — magnetic — it is feeding the sympathetic nervous system so called — Ida — name of this breath is — **Moon Breath** {breath of the sage ready to absorb wisdom}.

Coming, evenly thru both nostrils it is balancing = preserwing or destroying. It is called **Shushumna Breath**.

Normally the breath is changing about every hour — sun breath — neutral (Shushumna breath and moon breath.

Two postures create sun or moon breath in 3 (three) minutes. —

For the sun breath: lay down on the left side, and rest your head on the left hand. Thumb of which should be touching the cawity under left ear, rest of the fingers covering the forehead right arm is bent under straight angel, with the hand resting on earth with thumb pointing in the Assyrian manner.

Heel of the right foot lays on the knee joint of the left leg held straith. {By this posture it is changed in three minutes.}

The moon breath is the same posture reversed.
