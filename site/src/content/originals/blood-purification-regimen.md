Drink plenty water, fruit and vegetable juices. [Lemons, oranges, pineappes, prunes, apples, etc. - celery, onions, carrots, beets, cabbages etc.

Drink milk one hour or more after taking juices, after milk you can take juices two hours or after.

Use XI Arcane Mullah (Mullha) Mudra.

If you have to make the rejuvenation stronger, use juices of beets, celery, carrots, oranges and turnips, and after heating them little below body temperature, diluted in 50% water (together 1 quart) use as enema every three days. As to regular food, eat everything you like, or are used to, but in smaller quantities (on account of juices and milk that you are taking.
