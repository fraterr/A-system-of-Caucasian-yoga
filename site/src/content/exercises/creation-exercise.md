## In brief

The second Lesser Arcane — marked "G.", a great Arcane — is "used for awakening of the desire, and transmuting it into will power". Standing, with the fingers pressed into the pit of the stomach, you exhale fully, hold empty, inhale to capacity, lock the throat and shove the compressed air down against the fingers. The text says the jolt sends the energy of desire up the spine to the brain, where it becomes will.

## Technique

1. **Stand** erect, spine straight, head up, chin in. Relax and breathe deeply. Face the Sun, Moon, planets or stars "according to things you work for" (note beside the diagram). Eyes half closed throughout, in an "introspective gaze".
2. **Hands** over the solar plexus, at the ensiform cartilage (the lower tip of the breastbone), held there lightly, fingertips toward each other.
3. **Think of the thing you desire** and, slightly holding the breath, shake the diaphragm with short spasmodic movements — letting the breath in or out now and then — until you feel "the hunger and pang of the desire" in the solar plexus, often felt as heat.
4. **Exhale everything.** Relax the abdominal muscles absolutely and exhale all the air, pressing the fingers of both hands deep into the stomach at the sternum, between the ribs. As you do so lower the head until the chin rests on the chest (left figure of the diagram).
5. **Hold empty for seven seconds**, pressing the hands strongly in with a vibrating pressure and the chin into the chest, concentrating on "taking hold of the desire, as of a real living thing".
6. **Inhale slowly**, gradually lifting the head but not relaxing the digging pressure. Fill the lungs to capacity; by the end the head is lifted as for looking upward, "for praying" (right figure).
7. **Lock the throat** so that no air escapes. Give the compressed air a downward shove against the diaphragm, the solar plexus and the fingers: the pressure "should throw off the digging fingers with a snap".
8. **Release the hands.** Stop pressing; the text says you are now "in the subconscious and superconscious state" for as long as you hold the breath, and can use hands and body as necessary.

## What the text says it develops

- It awakens desire and transmutes it into will power, which "connects desire to the object of the desire, taking possession of it and making it come thru".
- It is "used in everything of importance": the author claims it materialises what you want, cures diseases, gives self-anaesthesia, and can bring trance, catalepsy or a lethargic, hibernating condition.
- It "arouses the serpent power of the body (Kundalini in Yoga)", consciously directing the same powers awakened in the Sixth Master Arcane.

## Phenomena and cautions

- Hunger, pang and heat in the solar plexus while vibrating the diaphragm.
- A sudden jolt in the solar plexus at the throat-lock; heat and pressure mounting within the spine; a snap at what the text calls "the pineal gland (medulla oblongata)", another between the eyebrows, and finally the top of the head.
- Tension pervading the head; thoughts disappearing one after another, "squeezed out of the brain", until only the thought of the desire remains.
- In the subconscious or superconscious state the eyes go out of focus (the margin says "converge") and objects are seen dimly; the text calls this normal.
- Caution: "working those powers is best beginning delicately, and being in practice with Major Master Arcanes".

## Notes for the reader

- The manuscript names the structure the energy strikes "pineal gland (medulla oblongata)", two different things; the card keeps the author's wording.
- Locking the throat, the text says, is what the Egyptian crux ansata, the Indian and Tibetan noose and the Persian "tie that binds" represent. The whole process is symbolised in the caduceus of Hermes (one word here is uncertain, transcribed "[heat?]") and in the winged scarab of Egypt.
- The text's prerequisite is practice of the Master Arcanes generally; the Sixth is the one named as working the same powers. All seven are listed in the frontmatter.
- How many rounds to do, how long to hold the breath after the lock, and how to come out of the state are not stated.
