## In brief

The fourteenth Lesser Arcane is the author's method for "striving to attain the things in life". You sit facing the Sun, establish the sense of "I, I am", picture the thing you want until its inevitability dawns on you, then convert the wish into a blow: breath locked, fist tensed, brought down on the desk with the words "I demand" or "I command". A second, silent form with three hand positions is given for use in the presence of another person.

## Technique

1. **Sit** at a desk or table, facing the direction of the Sun. Relax.
2. **Realise yourself**: concentrate on "I, I am"; consider the attributes of the ego, "I think, I feel, I will". Feel yourself conscious in the centre of the brain, receiving impressions and turning them into expressions.
3. **Grasp the words** "I can", "I want", "I must", "I will" with a clear understanding.
4. **Picture the object** of your desire clearly and plainly, and feel the desire in the solar plexus.
5. **Wait** until "the realisation of the inevitableness of your desire" dawns on you; then invoke the password, which is determination.
6. **Inhale** deeply and hold the breath, locking it with the pharynx.
7. **Raise the right hand**, close the fist with the thumb covering the other fingers, and tense fist and arm.
8. **Strike**: suddenly and with power bring the fist down on the desk. At the moment of impact relax the fist, release the breath, and say with the exhalation "I demand" or "I command", stating the wish.
9. **Rebound**: let the fist bounce off the surface in a semicircle toward yourself and finish with a short, sharp semicircle, also toward yourself. You strike with the little-finger side of the fist.
10. **Body**: as you pound, bend slightly forward and let the solar plexus, haunches and head "jump" slightly in response to the stroke.
11. **Repeat** for some time, according to the importance of the wish.

### The silent form (on a person directly)

Keep the inner work the same but do it mentally; an unnoticeable pressure of the hands replaces the stroke. Three positions:

1. **First position**: the right hand grasps the left wrist, the right palm over the back of the left hand.
2. **Second position**: the right hand grasps the outer side of the left hand, the right thumb pressing the outside of the left wrist, the other fingers pressing into the hollow of the left palm.
3. **Third position**: bend the fingers of both hands and hook them together so the bent fingers touch on their inside, the thumbs outside on the knuckles.

Speak the command in a quiet voice, charged with power, stressing the words that convey the wish exactly; while commanding, tighten the right hand imperceptibly on the left. In the third position press with the thumbs on the knuckles of the finger whose quality you want to awaken: thumb — will; index — destiny, command; middle — teacher, conveyor; fourth — humane feelings, sympathy, altruism; little — sex, carnal desire.

## What the text says it develops

- Pounding with the breath held "shakes the solar plexus", transmuting the image of the desire into the head, where it is "willified".
- The wish goes out "in the form of powerful invocation (command)... as ripples on the ether".

## Phenomena and cautions

- Expect the jolt in solar plexus, haunches and head; the text treats it as part of the mechanism.
- Do the pounding form alone; it is "not necessary of ever using this arcane in front of others".
- On a human being directly, the arcane changes form entirely: mental work and hand pressure only.

## Notes for the reader

- "Password" is not a spoken word here: the text says the password is determination.
- "I demand" and "I command" are the only formulas; the wish itself is stated in your own words.
- The meaning of each finger repeats the doctrine of the hands (hands-and-fingers).
- The description of the first position is garbled in the manuscript ("upper part of the right hand exposed, the palm covering the upper part of left hand"); this card reads it with the diagram.
- The manuscript's Conclusion ("Illumination") returns to the theme: "Desire, wish and will, order, demand command."
