## In brief

"Now you can do crystal gazing. For clairvoyance and also to tranmute the thoughts at a distance." After the First Master Arcane you rest the face in the hands and gaze into a crystal lit by a candle from behind against a black screen, splitting the ether lightly until the candle shows two reflections. A closed-eye variant is given for reading thought forms, an active variant for telepathy.

## Technique

1. **Sit** on a chair, relax, and do the First Master Arcane (twelve Master breaths on the four colours — filled in from that card).
2. **Rest the elbows** on a table; rest the upper face on the palms and fingers, thumbs placed behind the ears.
3. **Set the crystal** on a stand a little higher than eye level: glass ball, tourmaline, beryl, magical mirror, glass ball filled with water, "also fire".
4. **Light a single candle** behind you; behind the crystal have a dark screen, preferably black velvet.
5. **Gaze and concentrate** in the crystal, splitting the ether lightly until you see two reflections of the candle.
6. **Watch patiently**: "the things will begin to appear, and get clear". Five to thirty minutes without blinking, every day at the same hour; "follow the Sun".
7. **Thought forms.** Or hold the right hand over the face, pressing slightly on the top of the bridge of the nose, eyes closed; things appear outlined on the dark screen, "very often symbolic".
8. **Telepathy.** Use the same means, but instead of being receptive ("passive, blank") repeat the formula you want to convey and tune to the active state — will power and desire.

## What the text says it develops

- Clairvoyance; transmission of thoughts at a distance.
- Reading of thought forms (closed-eye variant); telepathic transference (active variant).

## Phenomena and cautions

- The doubled candle marks the right degree of splitting; then forms appear and clear. The author's one rule is regularity.

## Notes for the reader

- The posture resembles the Aum exercise, but here the thumbs go behind the ears, not into them.
- "(Folow the Sun.)" is not explained here; elsewhere the system means facing the Sun's direction.
- The telepathy note is marked "(x)" in the manuscript.
