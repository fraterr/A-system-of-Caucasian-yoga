## In brief

"Next step in developing of the eyes, is to learn the distance adjustment and gain conscious control over it." Two drills: keeping the eyes focused on a distance after the object there has been taken away (the inner gaze), and keeping a far focus while something near is put in the way, so that you "practically look thru the near object" (the far-away gaze).

## Technique

**Inner gaze**

1. **Hold an object** not far from the eyes and let the sight adjust to it.
2. **Remove it quickly**, but keep the eyes adjusted to the distance where the object was. Things beyond will seem hazy.
3. **Practise** until you can adjust the gaze at will.
4. **Dust.** Watch the dust particles suspended in the near air, and "feel beyond them" without changing the adjustment. The text: "This develops the inner gaze."

**Far-away gaze**

5. **Look at a very far object.**
6. **Put something much nearer in the way** without changing the far-seeing adjustment. You will practically look through the near object.
7. **Practise** "until you can look thru things". The text: "This is far away gaze."

## What the text says it develops

- Conscious control over the eyes' distance adjustment.
- The inner gaze and the far-away gaze, both named in the chapter's closing list ("inward sight, far away gaze") among the fruits of the Seventh Arcane.

## Notes for the reader

- The text gives no duration or time of day.
- Crystal gazing, which follows this exercise in the manuscript, begins "Now you can do crystal gazing", so the author treats these gazes as its preparation.
