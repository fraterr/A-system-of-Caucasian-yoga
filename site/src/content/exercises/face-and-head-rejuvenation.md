## In brief

The Twelfth Arcane is "complementary to the XIth Arcane" (Mullah Mudra). It keeps the kneeling posture, drops the tube and rectal breathing, and becomes a pump for the blood of the head: five minutes of strong breathing, then a quarter of an hour of rising on the knees and prostrating, fifteen times or more, so that blood is alternately driven out of and rushed into the face and head. The text says it rejuvenates and cleans "the face, neck, head" and refreshes "brain, organs of hearing, taste, smell, sinuses", hair and teeth included.

## The author's theory

Several pages of physiology precede the exercise (their opening is lost with manuscript page 86). Anabolism builds living substance from food; catabolism burns it for energy. Cells live in blood or lymph, which feeds them and carries off their wastes. When that supply is poor, refuse accumulates in the cells, their membranes stretch and lose elasticity, such cells "fit badly together" and make wrinkles, salts settle in the arteries and high blood pressure follows. Creams and massage from outside only stretch the cells further. "The only medicine" is to restore "the dissolving and curing tone of the blood" and then direct it to the congested areas: first purify (the "Ist part", on its own cards), then move.

## Technique

1. **Prepare the blood** with the juice-and-milk regimen, Mullah Mudra, and the two thyroid exercises twice daily.
2. **Kneel** facing the Sun or its direction, spine straight, arms raised, looking slightly upward: the Mullah Mudra posture without the tube or rectal breathing.
3. **Breathe** very strongly and deeply for about five minutes, "until your face and body will start tingling, showing strong oxydisation of the blood". Then relax.
4. **Face the direction** — the text breaks off here (manuscript page 92 missing) and resumes with the count: in fifteen minutes "bend and straighten fifteen times or more".
5. **Rise** upright on the knees, arms overhead, swaying slightly backwards: the "first part", sending the blood away from head and face. (In Mullah Mudra this is the moment of the full inhalation; pairing the two here is the card's inference.)
6. **Prostrate**: bend forward, elbows and forearms on the ground, forehead down, hips raised: the "second part", sending the blood to head and face. (In Mullah Mudra the breath is held during the bend and released as the elbows touch the ground.)
7. **Repeat** the cycle fifteen times or more in fifteen minutes, roughly one a minute.

## What the text says it develops

- Rejuvenates and cleans face, neck and head, "getting away with all the congestions, stasis, and unhealthy conditions" there, hair and teeth included.
- Refreshes and strengthens the brain, the organs of hearing, taste and smell, and the sinuses.
- Flushes cells clogged by "bad metabolism" with oxygenated, purified blood, against wrinkles, salt deposits and arteriosclerosis.

## Phenomena and cautions

- The deep breathing is carried until "face and body will start tingling"; the author reads this as strong oxidation of the blood and says to relax then.
- The flow of blood to and from the head "is absolutely synchronic with the breath": it recedes on inhalation and circulates more strongly while the breath is held and on exhalation.
- His warning is against cosmetics, not the exercise: creams, lotions, hot and cold applications and massage "in time do more damage than help".

## Notes for the reader

- Two manuscript pages are missing from the facsimile. Page 86 falls between the remark on cosmetics and the physiology, which begins mid-argument. Page 92 falls inside the exercise proper: "Then facing the direction" is cut off and the text resumes at "fifteen (15) minutes you should bend and straighten fifteen times or more". What was said there about orientation, breath during the bending and the count is lost; the card fills the posture from Mullah Mudra and says so.
- The heading reads "XII G.S." where the neighbouring arcanes carry "L."; the manuscript does not explain the letters.
- Before closing, the author appends a series of practices, each on its own auxiliary card (purifying the blood, thyroid exercises, directing the blood, feet, hands, laying on of hands, neck massage, tongue stick), and names them all "the arcane and exercises for rejuvenation of the human body".
