## In brief

A note on the feet follows the Second Master Arcane, because "the great toe is connected with hearing (auditory nerve) and coordinates harmony and rhythm in the body". It covers washing, oiling, clothing and, above all, keeping the great toe alive by pulling and massaging it.

## Technique

1. **Bathe** the feet with soap and water, or use an alcohol rub. The text distinguishes cold water, "used for magnetic shock", from hot, "for electric amperage".
2. **Wading** in rivers, brooks, the sea, dewy grass, or even a tub, is called magnetic; **walking** on dusty roads, sand or earth is electric.
3. **Dry** the feet well and **rub** them well with the hands.
4. **Oil** them: sweet almond oil, coconut or cocoa butter; "crude oil and kerosene are good also". Never use animal fats other than lanolin (from sheep's wool) or butter.
5. **Pull the great toes.** Sit with the legs straight out, bend forward and grip both great toes, arms straight, and stretch and pull (see the drawing). Massage the great toe thoroughly "to prevent numbness" and "keep it responsive and alive".
6. **Clothe** the feet in silk (first choice), linen or wool, or cotton, and draw stockings or socks on over the cloth.
7. **Change** the foot cloths whenever the feet are moist and cold.

## What the text says it develops

- A responsive great toe, tied to hearing and to "harmony and rhythm in the body", the faculties the Second Master Arcane trains.
- Changing damp foot cloths, he says, "prevents colds, coughs, and affections of ears, eyes and throat, as well as many rheumatic conditions, to a great extent".

## Phenomena and cautions

The author's only warnings are the ban on animal fats other than lanolin or butter, and the instruction to keep the feet dry and warm.
