## In brief

The text says a person's breath, in its depth and rhythm, "shows the vibration in which the person is at a given time". To make an inner contact you match that breath; once in unison you can understand and influence the other by changing your own breath, and theirs follows. The same principle is extended to crowds tuned by singing, and to a person far away, using the night, relaxation and an object or effigy as the link.

## Technique

### A person present

1. **Watch** the rise and fall of the other person's chest.
2. **Breathe in unison** with it. The text says this puts you in contact.
3. **Lead**: once tuned in, consciously modify your own breath; the other's breath changes with yours, "creating the states desired".
4. **Master rhythm**: the highest rhythm is 7 seconds in, 1 second pause, 7 seconds out, 1 second pause; by this breath you tune yourself and others "on the Master thought and rhythm of the Geoic entity".
5. **Discretion**: do it without others noticing.

### A crowd

1. **Sing "Aum" together** to make many people vibrate in unison.
2. **Master rhythm for a crowd**: for five to fifteen minutes have them sing "Yat-Ha-Ah-Hu-Vai-R[a?]-O" on the exhalation and inhale for seven seconds.
3. **Choose the mantra by its effect**: long, sonorous mantras slow the vibration, calm, relax and bring serenity; quick ones tense and harden the brain, touch the psychic and sex, and tend to hysterical or fanatical states, with relaxation coming only afterwards as exhaustion. Most people are more prone to the quick kind, which is easier to achieve.
4. **Movement**: swaying with slow vibration; hand-clapping and foot-stamping with fast. Use simple tunes and rhythms.

### The kiss

The author notes that people who love each other tune in by kissing: a correct kiss is done holding the breath, so that afterwards the two start breathing in unison. Holding hands also helps tuning in.

### A person at a distance

1. **Choose the time** when the person is relaxed, best at night while asleep.
2. **Relax** yourself consciously and breathe quietly as if asleep, concentrating mentally on the person.
3. **Optional link**: hold something belonging to the person that carries "the imprint of person vibrations"; or make a figure representing the person, working with the constant thought and feeling that it is a true representation, and adorn it with the person's belongings. Treat the object as the person and follow "the thread (invisible tie)" that binds them to it.
4. **Tune in** by delicately modulating the rhythm of your breath; then take the lead and establish the rhythm you desire. Face the direction of the Sun.

## What the text says it develops

- Contact with another mind, and the ability to understand and influence "according to your will".
- Control of the mood of a gathering through sung rhythm.
- Contact at a distance: when tuned in, the effigy "will sparkle with the life of its own, will become this person".

## Phenomena and cautions

- The sign of success at a distance is that the representation seems alive.
- Fast collective vibration is described as damaging to the brain and to psychic balance.
- The author's closing restriction: act "only according to the highest spirit", conscious of acting with the highest will; the exercise "is not to be played with, and not for curiosity sake, do it to help".

## Notes for the reader

- One letter of the crowd mantra is uncertain in the manuscript: "Yat-Ha-Ah-Hu-Vai-R[a?]-O". The Magical Projection ritual gives "Yat-Ha-Ah-Hu-Vai-Rio", which may be the same word.
- "Geoic entity" is the author's term and is not explained here.
- The note on yawning (yawning-exercise) says the best way to send telepathic messages is on the yawning breath after full relaxation; it complements the distance method.
- The Master rhythm is spelled out in full by the author on page 113; the card repeats it as he gives it.
