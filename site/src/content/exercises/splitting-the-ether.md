## In brief

"Now comes the exercise used for splitting the ether, for clairvoyance." Two forefingers held at the distance of the pupils are gazed at until a composite third finger appears between them; then two candles until a third candle appears; then a person's eyes until a third eye appears between them. The text says the last "gives the very great power over human beings and animals".

## Technique

1. **Sit** straight and relaxed.
2. **Form the hands.** Close the left hand leaving the index finger outstretched. Cover it with the right so that three fingers of the right clasp the three folded fingers of the left; the right forefinger is outstretched, its tip touching the left forefinger; the thumbs touch.
3. **Separate the forefingers** to the distance between the pupils of your eyes.
4. **Hold the hands** about two feet from the eyes and look at the forefingers until you see between them the third finger, composite of the two, "having on its sides two fingernails".
5. **Study it** until you see it perfectly and it becomes "a absolute reality". Move the hands farther and nearer, keeping the image steady.
6. **Two candles**, three to six feet away and three to four inches apart: look until you see the third candle between them. Vary both distances.
7. **Two eyes.** Look into a person's eyes until you see the third eye between them; learn to keep it steady.

## What the text says it develops

- Clairvoyance — this is the exercise "for splitting the ether".
- With the third eye held steady in another's face, "the very great power over human beings and animals".

## Phenomena and cautions

- The composite finger, the third candle and the third eye; steadiness of the image is the measure of progress. No warnings are given.

## Notes for the reader

- The manuscript drops a word in the hand posture ("cover the left hand with the so that…"); the card reads "with the right", as the sentence requires.
- Crystal gazing uses the same splitting "until you will see two reflections of the candle".
