## In brief

The First Master Arcane is the template for all seven. Seated and relaxed, you breathe in the Master rhythm while imagining and "willing" a colour into two regions of the body at once. Three breaths on red, three on yellow, three on blue, three on white: twelve breaths, three minutes and twelve seconds. The text says it "establishes the Master rhythm" and "develops sight and clairvoyance".

## Technique

1. **Sit** on a chair, spine erect, neck straight, head up, chin in. Relax every muscle except those that keep the back and neck straight.
2. **Hands** rest on the thighs, fingers slightly separated so that each hand forms a V; index fingers at the point of the knees, thumbs curling toward the inside of the knees.
3. **Feet** three to six inches apart at the heels, opened to form a V; shins and thighs also form V's. (The text: "life attracts life, symbol V has concentrating power".)
4. **Eyes** level, fixed without strain on the Sun, the Moon, or a spot on a vertical wall or stand three to seven feet away at eye level.
5. **Note your breath**: is the air flowing mainly through the right nostril (Sun breath, "heating and electrical") or the left (Moon breath, "cooling and magnetic")?
6. **Fix the thought** on Gaya-Lhama, "the centralizing principle of life", by loving, thinking of and willing it.
7. **Empty the lungs** fully before the first breath.
8. **Red, three breaths.** Inhale for 7 seconds expanding the abdomen, drawing in red Gaya-Lhama. Hold 1 second. Exhale for 7 seconds while by thought and will fixing the colour red in the lower abdomen and sex and in the back of the head. Hold 1 second. Repeat twice more.
9. **Yellow, three breaths.** Same rhythm, expanding only the chest; on the exhalation fix yellow in the chest and the forehead.
10. **Blue, three breaths.** Expand chest and upper abdomen; on the exhalation fix blue in the solar plexus (abdomen, diaphragm) and the top of the head.
11. **White, three breaths.** Expand as for red (chest, diaphragm and abdomen); on the exhalation fix white in the arms, hands, legs, feet and face.

One breath = 7 + 1 + 7 + 1 = 16 seconds.

## What the text says it develops

- It "establishes the Master rhythm" in the body, the rhythm the whole system rests on.
- It "develops sight and clairvoyance".
- Charging the four regions in this "willful and conscious manner" is called the basic part of every Master Exercise.

## Notes for the reader

- Which region receives which colour is given twice in the manuscript (Introduction and Compendium) with small differences: the Compendium lists only "lower part of the stomach and back of the head" for red and adds "forelegs" and "forearms" to white. The card follows the wording inside the exercise itself.
- The Egyptian seated posture, the author notes, is the same as this one, "only in exoteric doctrine it was shown with limbs brought together".
- The general rule for all Master Arcanes: balance the number of times you practise in Sun breath and in Moon breath over the day.
