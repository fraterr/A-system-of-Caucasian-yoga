## In brief

Every Master Arcane begins by noting which nostril the breath flows through, and the practitioner must balance Sun-breath and Moon-breath practice over the day. These two lying postures are the tool for choosing: "two postures create sun or moon breath in three minutes". They belong to the "Breath is life" doctrine of the Introduction rather than to any single Arcane.

## Technique

1. **For Sun breath** (right nostril), lie down on the **left side**.
2. **Rest the head on the left hand.** The thumb touches the cavity under the left ear; the rest of the fingers cover the forehead.
3. **Bend the right arm** at a right angle, the hand resting on the earth "with thumb pointing in the Assyrian manner" (the drawing shows the hand flat, thumb extended).
4. **Keep the left leg straight** and lay the **heel of the right foot on the knee joint** of the left leg.
5. **Wait three minutes.** "By this posture it is changed in three minutes."
6. **For Moon breath** (left nostril), take "the same posture reversed": lie on the right side, head on the right hand, left arm bent, left heel on the right knee.

## What the text says it develops

- The right-nostril **Sun breath** is "creative electric breath", feeding the vasomotor system (Pingala), "the breath of the warrior ready to fight".
- The left-nostril **Moon breath** is "the regulating and mothering principle, magnetic", feeding the sympathetic nervous system (Ida), "the breath of the sage ready to absorb wisdom".
- Breath through both nostrils evenly is the balancing **Shushumna breath**, "preserving or destroying".
- The breath normally changes by itself about every hour.

## Phenomena and cautions

None given, beyond the three-minute claim itself.

## Notes for the reader

- The Moon version is given in one line as the reverse; the card spells out the mirror image.
- "In the Assyrian manner" is not explained in the text; the drawing is the only guide.
- Use it to enter a chosen breath before an Arcane, or to even the count between the two breaths as the Third Arcane's general note asks.
