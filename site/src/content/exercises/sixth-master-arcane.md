## In brief

The Sixth Master Arcane changes the rhythm. Kneeling on the balls of the feet and holding two upright poles, you take eight breaths of twenty-four seconds: seven in (Puraka), seven held (Kumbhaka), seven out (Rechaka), three held. During the exhalation, the text says, "the magnetic and electric and thermal forces blend and travel to the outer ring, pass out and then return, causing ecstasy and intense calm and poise". It is the one Master Arcane with a full passage of phenomena and cautions.

## Technique

1. **Stand** erect three feet from the back of a chair with straight upright poles, or from two staffs of bamboo or other material.
2. **Kneel** close to the poles, holding them, by balancing on the balls of the feet and bending back to keep the balance as you go down. Relax once kneeling. A margin note adds: neck and spine straight, chin in, all muscles relaxed; feet straight, at a right angle, or heels out (see the sketch).
3. **Hold** the back of the chair or the poles gently, and **empty the lungs** thoroughly but gently and easily. The margin note prescribes first "excitation of the functions by rapid panting", then driving all residual air out with trunk, abdomen and diaphragm; "breath may be sniffed, sobbed or waved in, but sighed out".
4. **Eyes** on a penny-sized spot level with them on a wall or screen, without straining or staring, shifting around its circumference.
5. **Inhale for 7 seconds** (Puraka), tightening the grip on the poles as you inhale.
6. **Hold for 7 seconds** (Kumbhaka, "to hold or master the breath"). While holding, exert a gentle pressure with the breath against the diaphragm.
7. **Exhale for 7 seconds** (Rechaka) gently, fully, with perfect control, squeezing out the last possible bit of air. While exhaling, relax the grip but let the hands still grasp the poles gently and hold on to them.
8. **Hold 3 seconds** empty, then repeat. Eight breaths in all.
9. **Remain** on the knees, or seated, for three minutes and twelve seconds after the exercise is complete.

## What the text says it develops

- The blending of "the magnetic and electric and thermal forces", which travel to the outer ring, pass out and return, "causing ecstasy and intense calm and poise".
- The trance state that may follow "is healing and gives the power of laying on of hands, or healing by so doing".

## Phenomena and cautions

The author says you will feel "heat waves and electric shocks at the base of the skull, in the cerebellum and inter-brain", and a "magnetic current" flowing up the spine into the medulla and a long list of brain structures, from the ventricles to the pituitary and pineal glands. You will hear "a pulsating sound like a bell or chime", feel the aura swell, and sometimes feel "beating or folding of wings" at the back of the skull, "the Ka or bird (Ba) of the Egyptian mythology". All this "is all right".

Two things call for stopping: "when things suddenly go dark, you stop" (or, if you go on, you will pass into a sleep or trance and "must not be disturbed until your Guardian Angel or Heavenly Father awakens you"); and "if your knees rise from the floor or body rises in the air, stop at once. You do not want levitation to occur."

## Notes for the reader

- **No colours are prescribed.** The Index lists "posture, breathing colors, exercise" for this Arcane, but the surviving text names none. The `colours` field is left empty rather than guessed.
- **Manuscript page 36 is missing**: page 35 ends with the timing and the diagram, page 37 begins the Seventh Arcane. The Index shows the lost page carried transmutation, the "Philosopher's Stone", the blending of auric spheres and a "caution as to surroundings", none of which survives.
- Step 6 (hold) is preceded in the manuscript by a passage blacked out by the author; the numbered sequence is complete without it.
