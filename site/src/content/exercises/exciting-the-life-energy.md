## In brief

The fourth Lesser Arcane is a single tensed breath, "a quick helping exercise" for when you are tired "physically, mentally, spiritually or psychically", before any task, or "to bring power to face audience of one or more". You inhale for seven seconds while tensing the whole upper body, vibrate the tensed muscles for seven seconds holding the breath, then let go with a sharp "Ha". The text says it brings alertness and the "magnetic and electric powers of the body" into play and strengthens "the penetrating quality of the eyes".

## Technique

1. **Sit or stand** and relax completely. Inhale and exhale deeply a few times, then exhale thoroughly. Spine erect, head up.
2. **Inhale for seven seconds**, tensing the upper part of the body as you breathe in: chest, shoulders, arms, forearms and hands (fold the fingers), neck and jaw.
3. **Hold for seven seconds** and vibrate the tensed muscles while holding.
4. **Exhale quickly**, relaxing completely, opening the mouth and saying "Ha".
5. **Do it once.**

## Timing

| Phase | Time |
|---|---|
| Inhalation, tensing the upper body | 7 s |
| Hold, vibrating the tensed muscles | 7 s |
| Exhalation with "Ha", relaxing | quick |

One breath; the text says to do it once.

## What the text says it develops

- Alertness, and the "magnetic and electric powers of the body" brought into play.
- A stronger "penetrating quality of the eyes".
- Currents sent to what the text calls "the pineal gland (medulla oblongata)", stimulating it: "This is the serpent within the spinal column, raising his head in attention, and swelling it."
- Relief from tiredness on any of the four planes, and power before a task or an audience.

## Phenomena and cautions

- When you tense the jaw you will hear in the ears "a sound like of silver chains"; the text says this "is to be expected".
- No warnings are given for this Arcane.

## Notes for the reader

- Only the rhythm and the tensing are prescribed: the text says nothing about colours, about the spot for the eyes, or about which nostril is breathing.
- The vibration of the tensed muscles is not described further. The author's "standing and tensing exercise" is the Second Master Arcane, which may be the model.
- "Pineal gland" and "medulla oblongata" are named together here as in the Creation Exercise, although they are different structures; the card keeps the author's wording.
- The "serpent within the spinal column" is the same image the Creation Exercise uses for the Kundalini; there the power is raised, here it only "raises his head in attention".
- The exercise sits naturally beside the later Arcanes on recharging nervous energy and rejuvenation of energy, which also work on the body's stores of power.
