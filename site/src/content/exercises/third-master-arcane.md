## In brief

The Third Master Arcane keeps the seat and twelve-breath rhythm of the First and adds a movement and a sentence: bend forward on each inhalation until the ribs touch the thighs, eyes on a spot on the ground; rise erect on each exhalation, eyes back to the spot at eye level; and say to yourself "Breath is life", stressing a different word at each phase. The text says it develops "good taste and judgement of distance" and holds the attraction of the "great centralising principle", forming "a field of magnetic force around me, my magnetic field or aura".

## Technique

1. **Sit** on a chair, spine erect, neck straight, head up, exactly as in the First Arcane. Relax every muscle except those that keep the back and neck straight.
2. **Two spots.** Fix one spot level with the eyes (or use the Sun, the Moon "etc."), and a second spot three to four feet away flat on the earth or floor.
3. **Note your breath**, Sun (right nostril) or Moon (left nostril).
4. **Hands** on the thighs forming V's as in the First Arcane, index fingers at the knees; **feet** with heels three to six inches apart, opened into a V, shins and thighs forming V's.
5. **Exhale all air.**
6. **Inhale for 7 seconds, bending forward** rhythmically, spine and neck kept in one line, shifting the eyes to the ground spot. Expand the abdomen, drawing in red Gaya-Lhama. While inhaling, concentrate on the life principle, "acknowledging" it, and say mentally "**Breath** is life".
7. **Hold 1 second** when the ribs touch the thighs, saying mentally "Breath **is** life": "fixing the principle of life by affirming".
8. **Exhale for 7 seconds, rising** erect to the starting posture, returning the eyes to the horizontal spot. Charge the lower abdomen and the back of the head with red, saying mentally "Breath is **life**": "realizing and affixing the principle of life".
9. **Pause 1 second**, then repeat for two more breaths on red.
10. **Continue** with three breaths on yellow, three on blue and three on white, "using the expansions as described in the First Master Arcane" (filled in: yellow to chest and forehead, blue to solar plexus and top of the head, white to arms, hands, legs, feet and face).
11. **Hold the thought** on Gaya-Lhama ("Ga-El-Lha-Ma") throughout, eyes moving between the horizontal spot and the earth spot.

## What the text says it develops

- "Good taste and judgement of distance."
- It holds "the constant attraction of the great centralising principle", which the author phrases as the thought: "I am breathing life in on my breath, and forming a field of magnetic force around me, my magnetic field or aura."
- The Index adds "understanding the great centralising principle" and the practice of noting, with every Master exercise, which breath you are in.

## Phenomena and cautions

The text gives no phenomena for this Arcane. Its one general rule is placed here: "with all the Master Arcanes, always note in what breath you are when beginning exercises, and watch to have an even number of exercises on every breath". If you practised in Sun breath in the morning, practise next time in Moon breath.

## Notes for the reader

- The mental sentence is written out only inside the first red breath. The card assumes, as the wording "repeat" and "using expansions as described" implies, that the three stresses of "Breath is life" accompany every one of the twelve breaths.
- Likewise the text names the regions to charge only for red (lower abdomen and back of the head); the other colours' regions are filled in from the First Arcane.
- The drawing's figures I and II show the erect position (start of inhalation, end of exhalation), III the bent position. The horizontal spot is the First Arcane's; the ground spot is new and gives the "judgement of distance".
