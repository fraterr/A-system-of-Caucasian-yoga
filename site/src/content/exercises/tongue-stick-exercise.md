## In brief

The last of the practices the author attaches to the Twelfth Arcane works on the stomach, bowels and sex organs through the tongue. A small wooden stick is laid on the tongue and pressed down hard with both hands for five to fifteen minutes; where on the tongue it presses decides which organs are reached. The author ends with an explicit caution against its use in pregnancy.

## Technique

1. **Make the stick**: wood, about ¾ inch wide, ¼ to ½ inch thick, 6 to 8 inches long, rounded at the end and along the edges, and well polished (see the drawing).
2. **Insert** it into the open mouth and lay it on the tongue.
3. **Press hard**, holding the stick with both hands, so that the rounded end bears down on the tongue.
4. **Choose the spot.** For the upper abdomen press against the middle of the tongue; for the bowels and the sex organs press deep toward the root of the tongue.
5. **Hold** for five to fifteen minutes. "It will hurt, but one has to stand it."

## What the text says it develops

- Cures "stomack conditions".
- Makes the bowels move.
- Improves "the general tone of sex organs".

## Phenomena and cautions

- Pain is expected and, the author says, must be borne for the full time.
- His caution, in his own words: "this exercise should not be done to a pregnant woman, as it would bring about miscarriage."

## Notes for the reader

- A word before "middle of the tongue" is illegible in the manuscript; the instruction itself is clear.
- The stick is a different implement from the tube of Mullah Mudra, though both are described as rounded and well polished.
- Work on the tongue appears elsewhere in the system: the tongue-rolling of the thyroid exercises in this same arcane, and the pulling and lengthening of the tongue in Kechara Mudra.
