## In brief

The first of the two exercises the author gives "besides yawning" for redistributing the spinal fluid. It reproduces the mechanics of a yawn in three stages and uses the sounds in the ears and the tears as proof that it is done right. The author calls it "the one most important key to energy and power" and asks you to study it in every way.

## Technique

1. **Tense the jaw** muscles on the sides of the jaw as if chewing, without clenching the teeth. Tense and relax, moving the lower jaw slightly forward and back. You should hear the ring of delicate silver chains in the ears. Twelve times or more.
2. **Open the mouth** as wide as possible, using the jaw muscles to the utmost, and close the eyes, pressing the eyelids strongly together. You should hear a rumbling in the ears and tears should come. Twelve times or more.
3. **Combine**: tense the jaw as if chewing while opening the mouth wide and shutting the eyes tight; as the mouth opens, inhale heartily through it until you feel satisfaction and fulfilment in the solar plexus. Twelve times or more.

## What the text says it develops

- Relaxes the entire nervous system, while giving it "the possibility to be able if necessary to tense again according to the new pattern for thought".
- "The one most important key to energy and power", to be discovered by working at and studying the exercise.

## Phenomena and cautions

- Silver-chain ringing (stage 1), rumbling and tears (stage 2), satisfaction in the solar plexus (stage 3) are the signs of correct execution. No cautions are given.

## Notes for the reader

- The sentence about relaxing the nervous system breaks across the page turn ("...and at the same time giving. / it the possibility..."); the sense is continuous.
- Repetitions are "according to need"; the text sets no time of day.
