## In brief

The fifth Lesser Arcane is healing by breath and word. After a few deep breaths you bring your mouth close to the affected part, your own or another person's, and blow each exhalation over it while singing, without voice, the word Yat-Ha-Ah-Hu-Vai-Rio and then Om, for a few minutes, and end with a whispered prayer. The text says it is "to heal the wounds, stop bleeding, remove pain, and set in healing process in the organs in the body".

## Technique

1. **Get easy access** to the affected part, yours or another's.
2. **Breathe deeply** for a few moments.
3. **Bring the mouth** one to three inches from the part to be attended to.
4. **Inhale through the nose, exhale through the mouth**, blowing the breath over the affected spot. At the same time sing, "in very hushed way (without the voice)", the word Yat-Ha-Ah-Hu-Vai-Rio and then Om, vibrating the Om to the end of the breath.
5. **Continue for a few minutes.**
6. **Whisper a prayer** to the Heavenly Father to send the healing power to the sick part and restore it to its normal state.
7. **Hot or cold.** If the affection is heating (a locally higher temperature), "blow the incantations on cold"; if it is cooling (a local anaemia), "breath them on hot". The text does not say how the breath is made cold or hot.
8. **Hands, if you can.** Lay the right hand on the body near the affection and the left hand on the opposite part.

## What the text says it develops

- Healing of wounds, stopping of bleeding, removal of pain.
- The setting-in of the healing process in the organs of the body.
- The affected part, the text says, "will hear and react accordingly" to the words conveyed to it.

## Phenomena and cautions

- No sensations are described for this Arcane.
- Caution: "Do not let the person being treated hear the words, but convey them to the afected part, which will hear and react accordingly."

## Notes for the reader

- Yat-Ha-Ah-Hu-Vai-Rio is a longer form of the Yat-Ha-Ah-Hu-Vo of the Ritual of the Pentagram, which the author glosses "The Will of the Lord is Power"; no separate meaning is given for the -Vai-Rio ending, and no gloss is given here.
- The page ends with a line in an unidentified script (Caucasian- or Georgian-like) flanked by ∴ signs, presumably the word in its original letters; the text does not explain it.
- "Anemia" (local anaemia) is the manuscript's term for a cooled, blood-poor part, as opposed to a heated, inflamed one.
- The number of breaths is not fixed: "do it for few minutes". The wording of the prayer is left to the practitioner.
- The hand placement (right hand near the affection, left on the opposite part) resembles the auxiliary practices on the magnetic and electric hands and the laying on of hands. The Doctrine of the Heart also says its two-finger gesture "is used in healing", and the Creation Exercise claims to cure "diseases yours and others".
