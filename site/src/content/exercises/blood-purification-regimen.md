## In brief

Before the kneeling exercise of the Twelfth Arcane can do its work, the author says, the blood that it will drive into the face and head must itself be cleansed. This "Ist part — purifying and strengthening of the blood" is a regimen rather than an exercise: plenty of water and juices, milk at a distance from them, Mullah Mudra, and, when a stronger effect is wanted, a warm vegetable-juice enema.

## Technique

1. **Drink** plenty of water and of fruit and vegetable juices. The author's list: lemons, oranges, pineapples, prunes, apples; celery, onions, carrots, beets, cabbages "etc."
2. **Separate milk from juices.** Take milk one hour or more after juices; after milk, wait two hours or more before juices again.
3. **Practise Mullah Mudra**, the Eleventh Arcane (breathing through the rectum in the prostrate posture), which the text says cleans the intestinal tract and lower bowel.
4. **For a stronger rejuvenation**, take the juices of beets, celery, carrots, oranges and turnips, heat them to a little below body temperature, dilute them half and half with water to make one quart in all, and use this as an enema every three days.
5. **Eat** your ordinary food, "everything you like, or are used to", but in smaller quantities, because of the juices and milk you are now taking.

## What the text says it develops

- Restores "the dissolving and curing tone of the blood", the precondition for directing the blood to congested and undernourished tissues.
- Helps, with the thyroid exercises that follow, to dissolve and eliminate salts and waste products from the body.

## Notes for the reader

- No quantities are given for the water, juices or milk; only the enema is measured (one quart of half juice, half water).
- The regimen is the first of the two halves of the arcane; the second half is the kneeling exercise on the parent card. The thyroid exercises, which the author places between them, have their own card.
