## In brief

The author calls this the exercise of "tremendous import" that "entitles one to be one of the Order of the Serpent", and says it cannot be explained without having practised the jaw and nape exercises. A held breath and a rising inner tension are used to find a pressure at the base of the skull; the aim is to tense and relax the medulla at will and to tell apart three points of pressure inside the head.

## Technique

1. **Sit or stand**, spine straight, head up, chin in. From the start, concentrate on the feeling of pressure at the back of the head.
2. **Inhale** and lock the air in the throat with the larynx.
3. **Tense inside** (the diaphragm) "as if you would want to have a stool".
4. **Raise the tension**: relax the lower bowel and tense the stomach; relax the stomach and tense the inside of the throat.
5. **Breathe** freely as soon as you need air; notice that the tension at the base of the skull can be kept while breathing.
6. **Study** it until you can tense and relax the medulla at will. Fingertips on the muscle between skull and neck show its degrees of tension; you can relax that muscle and still feel the pressure in the head.
7. **Relax** by moving the head back and forth and side to side, shaking the face right and left, and rolling the head limply on the shoulders.
8. **Three points**: learn to tense at the base of the skull, behind the point between the eyebrows, and on the top of the head; separate them and hold one at a time; tense the right and left sides within the head. Work slowly, diligently, persistently.

## What the text says it develops

- The pineal gland (medulla oblongata), and "straight path in selfrealisation and mastery".
- Control over thought, which is formed "electrically" in a magnetically tensed formation the medulla can tense or relax.
- The three points are "the keys and the locks to knowledge of yourself"; the soul, "I am", with "I think, I feel, I will", revolves between them.

## Phenomena and cautions

- Tension is recognised by a feeling of pressure within.
- Sideways shaking of the head means the energy is overflowing onto the ether: stop and relax.
- Always relax the inside of the head completely after exercising.

## Notes for the reader

- "Order of the Serpent" is not explained further; a word before "principle" is unreadable.
- No number of repetitions is fixed: "use this exercise often".
