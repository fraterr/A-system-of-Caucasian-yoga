## In brief

After the magnetic-electric exercise the author finishes what he has to say about laying on of hands. The rule is one of polarity: the right hand is "the giving and conveying hand" and goes on the suffering part; the left hand goes on the opposite side "as a receiving pole for the healing power". He adds how to raise the energy, when to work vigorously and when gently, and a small routine of pressing the fingertips to bring harmony into the body's processes.

## Technique

1. **Right hand** on the suffering part of the body: it gives and conveys.
2. **Left hand** on the side opposite the pain, as the receiving pole.
3. **For more energy**, before laying on, dry the hands well and heat them "by brisk friction of one against the other".
4. **Without fever, wounds or strained tissue**: the body may be kneaded and massaged vigorously, and the affected part pressed strongly.
5. **With fever, wounds or strained tissue**: lay the hand on lightly and "concentrate more on sending power delicately, to mend the broken tissues, and awaken them to the healing process".
6. **For harmony**, when exercising the hands: press the tip of every finger of one hand between the thumb and index finger of the other; press the palm as well, and especially the muscle between index finger and thumb, under the thumb.

## What the text says it develops

- The conveyance of "the healing power" from the practitioner to the sufferer through the polarity of the two hands.
- Relief of pain and the awakening of damaged tissues "to the healing process".
- The fingertip pressing is said to "bring harmony into body processes".

## Notes for the reader

- The opening phrase is marked uncertain in the manuscript ("Finishing about laying on of the hands [?]"), and a word is illegible in the sentence on pressing the fingertips.
- The current the hands convey is the one raised by the magnetic-electric exercise on the preceding pages; the earlier Lesser Arcane on healing power is the doctrinal background.
