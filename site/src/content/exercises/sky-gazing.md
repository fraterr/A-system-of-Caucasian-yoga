## In brief

The last of the eye exercises of the Seventh Arcane, given in one paragraph. Lying on the earth, you watch the blue sky and try to penetrate it as far as you can, and at night try to reach the stars. The author says this makes the eyes sensitive to ultraviolet and infra-red rays and "developes faculty of seing aura, of humans and other beings", as well as "psyching objects".

## Technique

1. **In nice warm weather lie down** on your spine on the grass, sand or earth.
2. **Fold the arms** behind the neck and head as a pillow.
3. **Watch the blue sky**, trying to penetrate as far as possible into it.
4. **At night**, do the same, trying to reach the stars.

## What the text says it develops

- Sensitivity of the eyes to ultraviolet and infra-red rays.
- The faculty of seeing the aura of humans and other beings.
- "Psyching objects" — what the chapter's closing list calls psychometry.

## Notes for the reader

- No duration is given.
- Lying on the earth is also the point of the sleeping rules in the Eighth Arcane (head to North), though the text does not connect the two.
- The far-away gaze, which trains looking "thru" near things, is the natural companion to penetrating the sky.
