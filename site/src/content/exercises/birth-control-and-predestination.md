## In brief

The sixteenth Lesser Arcane is short and is reported here as the text gives it. Intercourse, it says, "is the creation" and "the key to immortality". Two claims are made: that a sound made by the man at the moment of spending conveys the spark of life, without which conception does not take place; and that the sex of the child depends on which breath, Sun or Moon, each parent is in at the time, which in turn depends on the side each lies on.

## Technique

1. **The sound of conception.** At the moment of spending the man emits a sound, usually double: a deep intake of breath with a gasp or hiss, the breath held at the moment of spending, then an exhalation with a sigh or moan. The final sound of the exhalation, "Om", the text says, "conveys the spark of life from man into the woman" and prepares fertilisation; "without this sound the conceiving wont take place".
2. **Preparation.** Before intercourse the man and woman pet and caress each other lying side by side. The text says the sex of the child then depends on the breath each is in at the time of copulation, Sun or Moon, and it ties that breath to the side each lies on (Sun breath = right nostril, Moon breath = left nostril; see sun-and-moon-breath-posture).
3. **For a male child.** The man lies on his left side facing the woman, who lies on her right side. He is then in Sun breath (right nostril), she in Moon breath (left nostril).
4. **For a female child.** The man lies on his right side facing the woman, who lies on her left side. He is in Moon breath, she in Sun breath.
5. **Other combinations.** If both are in Sun breath, or both in Moon breath, the text says a male child will be effeminate or a female child masculine.

## What the text says it develops

- Control of conception: the arcane is headed "Birth control and control of the sex of the child to be born. Conception and predestination."
- Participation in "the equation started by the Ancient Ones": the creative act as the key to immortality.

## Phenomena and cautions

- The author gives no cautions. The only conditions stated are the sound at the moment of spending and the positions of the partners.

## Notes for the reader

- The letter of the final sound is partly unreadable: the manuscript reads 'Final sound of exhalation is "[B?]" ∴ "Om"'. This card takes "Om" as the intended word.
- "Birth control" in the heading is not developed: the text says only that without the sound conception does not take place; it gives no separate method of prevention.
- The emblem on page 117 (Sun, hexagram in a circle, Moon) closes the arcane; the text does not comment on it.
- The Lesser Arcanes end here; the manuscript continues with "Illumination (Conclusion)".
