## In brief

A short set of drills, given in one paragraph of the Seventh Arcane, "to control the muscles of your eyes". They train range, rolling, convergence, crossing and the ability to move the two eyes independently, and prepare the eyes for splitting the ether and the gazes that follow.

## Technique

1. **Roll the eyes**, both with them open and with them tightly closed.
2. **Strive to see as far back** of you as you can, and also as far up and down as you can, without moving the head.
3. **Roll the eyes in geometrical figures.**
4. **Converge and cross them**: look at the "Urna", the point between the eyebrows, then bring the crossed eyes down to the tip of the nose.
5. **Describe geometrical figures** with the eyes crossed; work until you can.
6. **Split the gaze**: develop the faculty of looking with one eye up and with the other down.

## What the text says it develops

- Control of the muscles of the eyes; the text gives no other effect for the drills themselves, but places them between sun gazing and the exercise "used for splitting the ether, for clairvoyance".

## Notes for the reader

- The convergence toward the point between the eyebrows is the same eye position used in concentration itself and in the Aum exercise ("looking on the mountain tops. Toward the urna").
- No number of repetitions, duration or time of day is given.
