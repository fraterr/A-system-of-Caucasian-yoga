## In brief

The Fourth Master Arcane is done standing, feet wide apart, one arm at a time. In the **first part** the raised arm circles rapidly before the eyes, one revolution a second, seven on the inhalation and seven on the exhalation, cutting the line of sight at every turn. In the **second part** the arm rises over the shoulder with the fist clenching as you inhale, strikes the earth in the one-second pause, and sweeps back up as you exhale. Each colour gets both parts with one arm, then with the other. The text says it "is developing the commanding will, and electricity, storing it in ganglias of the body ready to use".

## Technique

1. **Stand** erect, spine and neck straight, feet eighteen to twenty-four inches apart.
2. **Raise one hand** above the eyes, about 18 inches from them, palm toward you. Keep the **eyes on the spot** at eye level, the Sun or the Moon; thought and will fixed on Ga-El-Lha-Mah. **Note your breath**: the exercise always begins with the arm on the side in which the breath is flowing (right arm in Sun breath, left in Moon breath).
3. **Exhale all residual air** using the muscles of the abdomen, diaphragm and chest.
4. **First part, inhalation.** Rotate the arm rapidly, one revolution a second, **toward the body**, so that it cuts off the vision at every circle. Breathe in red Gaya-Lhama for 7 seconds while making seven circles, letting the arm carry its own momentum.
5. **Drop the arm** limp to the side: this is the 1-second pause.
6. **First part, exhalation.** Raise the same hand and arm again and exhale for 7 seconds while rotating seven times, charging the lower abdomen and the back of the head with red. At the end let the arm drop to the side by its own momentum. Pause 1 second.
7. **Second part, inhalation.** Inhaling for 7 seconds, raise the same arm full length over the shoulder, clenching the hand and gradually tensing the grip (red, physical).
8. **Tense and slightly vibrate** the clenched hand for the 1-second pause. Then **bend rapidly and strike** the earth in front of the feet with the fist, letting go of breath and grip at the moment of the strike.
9. **Second part, exhalation.** Raise the body erect, throwing hand and arm up, back and down to the side in one round, graceful motion, timing motion and exhalation at 7 seconds, charging red into the lower abdomen and back of the head; the grip is relaxed on the way up. Pause 1 second.
10. **Repeat both parts** on the same colour with the **other arm**.
11. **Continue** on yellow, blue and white, four breaths each. The text names regions only for red; filled in from the First Arcane: yellow to chest and forehead, blue to solar plexus and top of the head, white to arms, hands, legs, feet and face.

The text states sixteen breaths, "four on a colour, two on the breath and two complimentary"; the total time is my computation at 16 seconds a breath and is not given by the author.

## What the text says it develops

- "The commanding will."
- "Electricity, storing it in ganglias of the body ready to use."

## Phenomena and cautions

No phenomena are described. The cautions are procedural: empty the lungs first; start on the side of the flowing breath and rotate toward the body; release breath and grip exactly at the strike; pass from the first part into the second with only "the regular intermediate one second" so that the two remain one exercise.

## Notes for the reader

- "Two on the breath and two complimentary" is read as two breaths (both parts) with the arm on the side of the active nostril, then two with the other arm; the text confirms this order explicitly for the second part.
- The author remarks that the profile posture of the first part "can be recognised in the Egyptian system".
