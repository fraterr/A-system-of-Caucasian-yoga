## In brief

The Fifth Master Arcane is the "flying" exercise. Knees slightly bent, the arms float out level with the shoulders, hands limp from the wrists; the arm muscles tense on each inhalation (never the hands) and the arms fold on the breast on each exhalation. Two breaths per colour are taken with the arms in front, one with the arms out at the sides; the twelfth exhalation is thrown out through the mouth on a long sighing HÂ. The text says this Arcane "gives control over attraction of the Earth (weight), enabling to raise in the air, fly and walk on water".

## Technique

1. **Stand** erect, spine and neck straight, feet three to six inches apart, **knees slightly bent**. Relax all muscles.
2. **Note your breath**, Sun or Moon. Keep the **eyes** on the Sun, the Moon or the spot; thought and will fixed on Ga-El-Lha-Mah.
3. **Raise the hands and arms** from behind, over the head, down to the front, level with the shoulders. "Float" them on the air as if about to fly, hands limp from the wrist.
4. **Exhale all residual air** with the abdominal, diaphragmatic and chest muscles, and fix the thought on red.
5. **Inhale 7 seconds**, tensing the arm muscles as far as the wrists only, leaving the hands limp, expanding the lower abdomen for red. Pause 1 second.
6. **Exhale 7 seconds**, relaxing and **folding the arms on the breast**, right hand on the right breast, left hand on the left, while charging the lower abdomen and back of the head with red. Pause 1 second.
7. **Repeat** for a second breath in front on the same colour.
8. **Extend the arms to the sides** at shoulder level, tensing the arm muscles (hands limp) on the inhalation, folding on the breast on the exhalation, once on the same colour.
9. **Continue** with yellow, blue and white in the same way: two breaths with the arms in front, one with the arms at the sides. Regions for the other colours are filled in from the First Arcane: yellow to chest and forehead, blue to solar plexus and top of the head, white to arms, hands, legs, feet and face.
10. **Last breath.** Return the arms to the front, swing the hands up with the fingers pointing toward each other but not touching, then drop them and **exhale through the mouth quickly, saying HÂ** "as in HA HA", but as one long sighing breath, the arms swinging limp "as the lesson goes".

The author gives the count (twelve breaths) but not the time; 3 min 12 s assumes the Master rhythm of 16 seconds a breath.

## What the text says it develops

- "Control over attraction of the Earth (weight)."
- The ability "to raise in the air, fly and walk on water".

## Phenomena and cautions

No phenomena are described for this Arcane. The author's only insistences are on the posture: knees slightly bent, hands always limp, tension in the arms only up to the wrists, all residual air emptied before the first breath.

## Notes for the reader

- The description comes in two passes that disagree: first "on every colour three times (12 breaths)" in front, then the sides "on every colour (three times)"; the author then corrects himself, "to explain correctly, on every colour there are three breaths, two with hands in front and one with hands on the sides". The card follows the correction.
- The HÂ exhalation is attached to "the last breath" after returning the hands to the front. Whether it replaces the twelfth 7-second exhalation or follows it as an extra release is not stated; the wording ("drop and exhale through mouth quickly") suggests it is the final exhalation itself.
- The profile figure in the drawing shows the movement of one arm only, for clarity.
