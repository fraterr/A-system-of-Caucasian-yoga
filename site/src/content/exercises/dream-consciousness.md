## In brief

The Eighth Arcane aims at what the author calls the third state of consciousness: the dream state, "when one is fully awake outside of the body, during the sleep". The method is two long days of practice a week apart. On each you sit with the face in the hands and the thumbs in the ears and sing a mantram in a low voice for three sessions of two hours, resting two hours between them, then sleep. The first day is without liquids, the second without food. The text says posture, fasting and the vibration of the singing "impresses the subconscious and the soul, bringing in realisation of dream consciousness".

## Technique

**First day**

1. **Set aside a day** completely to yourself, in a place free from outside disturbances. Face the direction of the Sun; be relaxed and comfortable.
2. **Sit** on a chair at a table with a pillow on it. Bend toward the table so the elbows rest on it, spine straight.
3. **Rest the upper face and forehead** on the palms, fingers a little spread. Wet the thumbs and insert them into the ears.
4. **Close the eyes** and turn them slightly upward — the posture is "exactly like" the Sixth Lesser Arcane, the Aum.
5. **Sing** in a low voice, incessantly, on a mantram tune: "Huong, Yang, Yang, Yang – Huong, Yang, Yang – Huong, Yang Yang, Yang – Houng, Yang, Yang". Two hours without variations, ears stopped.
6. **Rest two hours.** Solid food if hungry; no water, liquids or liquid food all day.
7. **Repeat** to make three sessions of two hours.
8. **Rest and sleep.** At first it is hard to fall asleep, "but it will come"; during sleep you will hear the mantram.

**Second day, one week later**

9. **Proceed exactly as before** with another mantram and another tune. This day you may drink water but must take no food.
10. **Rest and sleep.** You will hear that day's mantram; then the mantram of a week ago will occur to you, and you will recognise it and suddenly understand which is which. The text says this recognition "will give orientation in your actions", bringing "consciousness in your dreaming state".

**Sleeping**

11. **Head toward North** at night, always; the text says this strengthens the body's currents with the Earth's magnetic field and the Sun's electric currents. When not well, "give shock to your energies" by sleeping North to South.
12. **Side.** For energy on the physical and spiritual planes fall asleep on the left side (Sun breath, positiveness); for study or peaceful endeavours on the right (Moon breath, passiveness; intellectual and psychic planes). Balance the two.
13. **Conscious in sleep**, use careful observation, correct interpretation and practical application; "the Silver Cord will guide you back". Act according to "Yat-Ha-Ah-Hu-Vai-Rio", "The Will of the Lord is the Law of Righteousness".

## What the text says it develops

- Awakening in the dream: being conscious during sleep, able to "move around in the astral body, learn things, be able to perfom things".
- Through this, the "Occult state of consciousness" and the further states the author lists: mental, spiritual, astral, super, self and cosmic.

## Phenomena and cautions

- Difficulty falling asleep after the first day; the mantram heard in sleep; on the second night the recognition of the earlier mantram, which the text treats as the turning point.
- The author's restrictions: no liquids on the first day, no food on the second, a full week between the two.

## Notes for the reader

- The tunes of both mantras are missing: the manuscript leaves two pairs of empty staves with a typed note that "the music for the mantras on pages 74 and 75 was not furnished with the manuscript". The words of the second mantram are not given either.
- "Yat-Ha-Ah-Hu-Vai-Rio" is followed by a line of characters in an unidentified script.
