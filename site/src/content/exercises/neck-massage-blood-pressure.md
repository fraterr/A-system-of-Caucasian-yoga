## In brief

A single sentence in the manuscript, placed between the laying on of hands and the tongue stick. The author has argued earlier in this arcane that hardened arteries bring high blood pressure and "general debility"; here he gives a manual means of bringing the pressure back to normal, without saying in which direction it is expected to move.

## Technique

1. **Place the fingers** of both hands on the sides of the neck, below the back of the ears.
2. **Massage** thoroughly, pressing on the spot and working with circular motions.

## What the text says it develops

- "To change the blood pressure, making it normal."

## Notes for the reader

- No duration, number of repetitions or time of day is given.
- The exercise belongs with the author's general teaching on controlling the blood: he has just explained that pressure on veins or arteries lying close under the skin checks or frees the flow, and the neck below the ears is one of the places where large vessels do run near the surface. He does not make the connection explicit here; that is the reader's inference.
- The sentence follows immediately on the fingertip-pressing paragraph of laying on of hands and precedes the tongue-stick exercise, all on the same manuscript page.
