## In brief

The third Lesser Arcane, marked "G." and, in the margin, "Master projection", is a projection of power. After twelve seated Master breaths you stand facing North and, with the index finger of the closed right hand, draw a pentagram in the air in five strokes sung to the five syllables of Yat-Ha-Ah-Hu-Vo, then a circle and an inward spiral ending in a forward thrust on Om. The text says it builds "the wall of absolute protection against adverse powers and thoughts" and is also "a powerful and terrible weapon to strike and destroy the enemies".

## Technique

1. **Face North** and sit. Do the twelve complete breaths of the First Master Arcane in the Master rhythm: 7 seconds inhalation, 1 second stop, 7 seconds exhalation, 1 second stop, twelve times. The text names only the rhythm; whether the four colours of the First Arcane are to be used is not said.
2. **Stand** upright, head up, chin in, right foot forward: the standing posture of the Second Master Arcane.
3. **Inhale deeply.** Close the right hand with the index finger pointing, and keep it so throughout, "as if writing in the air".
4. **First motion, "Yat".** Move the right arm across to the left and, from your left side, swing a stroke upward to the apex of the pentagram, straight over your head.
5. **Second motion, "Ha".** Swing the arm downward toward the right side (the lower right point).
6. **Third motion, "Ah".** Swing the arm up toward over the left shoulder (the upper left point).
7. **Fourth motion, "Hu".** Swing horizontally across to over the right shoulder (the upper right point).
8. **Fifth motion, "Vo".** Bring the arm down from the upper right toward the lower left; this stroke closes the pentagram.
9. **Sixth motion, "Om".** Without stopping, swing the arm in a wide circle around the pentagram, then continue half a circle toward the centre while stepping forward with the right foot and making a thrust with arm and hand, forefinger pointing. The circle and half circle form a spiral "drawn in the air from left to right". Sound "Om" through the spiral and the thrust, spending all the rest of the breath on the humming end of the sound.
10. **Drop** the hand and arm, relaxed, to the side.
11. **Repeat** "according to necessity", up to twelve times, always facing North.
12. **Protective wall (variant).** To build the wall of protection, make the figure "to the four corners of the Earth, above and below", using only the pentagram, without the spiral and thrust.

## What the text says it develops

- Protection: "the wall of absolute protection against adverse powers and thoughts".
- Power to strike: "a powerful and terrible weapon to strike and destroy the enemies".
- The words: Yat-Ha-Ah-Hu-Vo means, the author says, "The Will of the Lord is Power" and "is the word that rooster knows"; Om "is the word that lion roars".

## Phenomena and cautions

- The text describes no sensations for this Arcane.
- Caution, in the author's words: "use this only when you know that it is the only way, to act in accordance with the Spirit of the Law".

## Notes for the reader

- Path of the pentagram, read from the text and the eight step drawings: lower left → apex → lower right → upper left → upper right → lower left. The first stroke starts at the left side of the body and the fifth returns there.
- The last two drawings are labelled "Complete" and "Complete with Zend mantram", the latter with Avestan-like characters around the ring; page 64 ends with two lines in an unidentified script which the text does not explain. "Zend" is the author's pointer to the Avestan origin he gives the word.
- "Sing" is the author's verb; how loud is not said. In the Healing Power Arcane the related word Yat-Ha-Ah-Hu-Vai-Rio is sung "without the voice".
- No left-handed or Moon-breath variant is given, although in the Second Master Arcane the forward foot depends on the breath (right in Sun, left in Moon).
- The twelve seated breaths read as done once at the start, not before each projection; the text does not say.
