## In brief

The first Lesser Arcane is a seated meditation on the heart. With two fingers of one hand resting on the heart you follow the heartbeat and pair a word with every beat: first "love", then the "holy word" Al-Im, and finally "peace". The text says that this doctrine "is the development of love and discrimination", that the heart "should be consulted in every important undertaking", and that the same gesture "is used in healing".

## Technique

1. **Sit** in a quiet place facing the Sun, the Moon or a planet (in its direction). Breathe deeply, then relax and "withdraw within yourself".
2. **Fold the hand** so that only the index and middle fingers are outstretched — the text calls them "destiny and teacher" — and lay the two fingers on the heart. This is the "sacerdotal hand" of the diagram. Which hand is not said here; the later instruction to cover "the right hand" with the left implies you begin with the right (filled in).
3. **Watch the heartbeat** and consciously fill the heart with love, repeating the word "love" with every beat. The text allows the word "lob" instead, calling "lob and dob" the two sounds of the heart (systole and diastole), from which "love" derives.
4. **Let the feeling grow.** The text says you gradually become conscious of love concentrating in the heart, as a "sensation of congestion", a pressure of feeling and fullness in the cardiac region.
5. **Change hands** when the feeling reaches its "pinnacle of tension": cover the right hand gently with the left, slowly withdraw the right hand from under it, and shape the left hand as before, index and middle fingers pointing to the heart, the other fingers closed.
6. **Utter Al-Im** with every beat. This, the text says, "is the holy word which opens the heart". As the word is repeated you "enter the interior of the heart": it is filled with red clouds and mist; in the centre stands "the arc, with measures of discrimination", and over it the blazing pentagram with the all-seeing eye in its centre (the diagram on page 57).
7. **Worship and commune**, and send out love "to understand, reach, help and bless".
8. **Close** by returning to the opening position: put the right hand on the heart as at the beginning, withdraw the left, and with index and middle fingers on the heart repeat "peace" with every beat, to fill the heart with it. This, the text says, "completes the Doctrine of the Sacred Heart".

## What the text says it develops

- Love and discrimination: the doctrine is "one of the greatest signs of the fulfilment and evolution".
- A means of consultation: "Heart should be consulted in every important undertaking", by placing on it the two fingers of the left hand.
- A healing use: the gesture "also is used in healing" (compare the Healing Power Arcane and the laying on of hands).

## Phenomena and cautions

- Congestion, pressure and fullness in the cardiac region as the feeling of love concentrates.
- An inner vision of the heart's interior: red clouds and mist, the "arc" with "measures of discrimination", the blazing pentagram and the all-seeing eye.
- The text gives no warning for this Arcane.

## Notes for the reader

- The preamble on page 55 sets the exercise in a doctrine of two paths, symbolised by the two hooked crosses: the Souwastika (the manuscript's spelling), used in the Orient, for the "closed heart but open mind", developing reason; and the Swastika, used in the Occident, for the "open heart and feelings", bringing wisdom. A cross-section of the heart, the author adds, shows its muscles in these two forms.
- "The arc, with measures of discrimination": the word may be "arc" or a spelling of "ark"; the manuscript's spelling is kept and the sense left open.
- No timing is given — no number of beats, breaths or minutes for any phase; the only cue for moving on is the "pinnacle of tension" of the feeling of love — and no breath rhythm beyond "breathe deeply, then relax" at the start.
