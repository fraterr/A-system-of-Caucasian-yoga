## In brief

"Magical Projection. Invocation of the host of angels or powers." This is the last section of the manuscript and it is incomplete: the text stops in the middle of a sentence on page 127, where a later hand has written "end" and "breaks off". What survives is the setting (time, direction, circle, altar, wand), the words of protection, the author's account of how invocation works, and the beginning of the instructions for invoking an elemental. No step-by-step ritual is given; the card reports what is there.

## Technique

1. **Charge** your body first according to the First Master Arcane.
2. **Confirm** the "I" and "I am".
3. **Stand** at midnight facing North, in a circle with a square inside or outside it: "square and the circle are opening the way on the infinity".
4. **Draw the circle** with chalk or carbon held in the right hand, while the left fist is tightly closed with the thumb covering and pressing the index and middle fingers. Draw from left to right, following the movement of the Sun. Around the circle inscribe the name or names and words of protection suited to the ritual, writing them with thought, feeling and will.
5. **Words of protection**: "Yat-Ha-Ah-Hu-Vai-Rio", the Master Protection, "The will of the Lord is the law of righteousness"; or "Yat-Ha-Ah-Hu-Vo", "The will of the Lord is might". Both are also written in the author's script.
6. **Altar**: in front of you, of marble, wood or metal, bearing the same design as the circle, engraved, etched, painted or drawn. It "represents the field on which the actual work will be done in scale".
7. **Wand**: wood hollowed and fitted with a magnetised steel wire, the North pole in the hand and the South pole at the tip, with a tourmaline crystal attached; a copper wire wound round it in a right-handed spiral, ending in a copper plate that helps hold the tourmaline. Length: an arm or a forearm.
8. **Orientation**: work "according to the magnetic pole and electric sun"; the two diagrams on page 124 set altar and circle in relation to the North Pole and the Sun.
9. **Invoke**: centre the thought on one point, endow it with feeling, arm it with will. Sent this way, the text says, the thought becomes "crystallographic axis" around which the powers gather, build it up and materialise, "growing in strength untold number of times", under the formula "As above, so below; as below, so above".
10. **For an elemental**: awaken its spirit essence in yourself and project it into the space outlined for it outside the circle, a triangle with an inscribed circle whose centre is "the point of concentrative projection"; the hierophant's wish, desire and will is the axis around which the image of the entity is manifested, "like attracts alike".

## What the text says it develops

- Invocation of "the heavenly host": powers of the same vibration are attracted and become "obedient to the conscious thought which called" them.
- The making of "loaded with feelings and willified thought", which the author calls "the work of magick".

## Phenomena and cautions

- Your own emanations are the danger: the circle protects you from them, since they "grow to the extent of being very dangerous when they try to take possesion of your body and mind".
- The conscious thought is the leader and can rebound; you must be protected and strong enough to withstand it.

## Notes for the reader

- The manuscript breaks off mid-sentence: "Ritual, ceremonial, candles, in" is the last line. The ritual proper, what is said and done once the setting is prepared, is not in the text.
- "Yat-Ha-Ah-Hu-Vai-Rio" is probably the same word as the "Yat-Ha-Ah-Hu-Vai-R[a?]-O" of the Mental Connection arcane; the author uses "Yat-Ha-Ah-Hu-Vo" and related forms elsewhere (see sacred-words).
- Two small boxes in the text ("[small box]") mark places where the author left room for something, perhaps a figure.
- Compare the Ritual of the Pentagram (III G.) for the author's other ritual of projecting power.
