## In brief

"Great influence on the human body is exerted by the feet and the toes." With this the author turns from the blood to the extremities. He recalls the Second Master Arcane, whose standing and rising on the balls of the feet he calls of "very serious importance", and adds a short routine of massage and movement for the feet. Its purpose, he says, is to reach the nerve centres and, through them, the glands.

## Technique

1. **Massage** the feet thoroughly.
2. **Knead** the ankles and all the muscles of the feet, exercising them.
3. **Pull the toes**, massaging them as you go.
4. **Press the tips** of the toes, "especially the great toe".
5. **Move the ankles** around, up and down, and sideways, with vigour.
6. **Do the same with the toes**: move them in every direction.

## What the text says it develops

- Pressing the toe-tips "will awaken nerves" and bring about an increased stimulation of the blood.
- It reacts beneficially on the nerve centres and ganglia, and through them stimulates "the different glands in the body".
- Together with the Second Master Arcane it puts to use the influence that the feet and toes exert on the whole body.

## Notes for the reader

- The passage is a single paragraph without timings or repetitions; the author gives the elements and leaves the dose to the practitioner.
- It should be read with the earlier auxiliary on the care of the feet and with the Second Master Arcane, which the author names here as the exercise that gives the feet their importance.
- The word transcribed as "strivlation" of blood is unclear in the manuscript; "stimulation" fits the context.
