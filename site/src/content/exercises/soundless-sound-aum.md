## In brief

The sixth Lesser Arcane is listening for the "soundless sound", the "Voice of the Silence": "the hum of the rhythm of the world", the sacred Aum. Seated at a table with the face resting in the hands and the ears sealed, after twelve Master breaths you turn the closed eyes up toward the point between the eyebrows and give your whole attention to the inner left ear. The text describes a sequence of inner sounds — blood, trumpet, bee, bell, flute — and, after a lull, the Aum. "Listen to it — you will understand."

## Technique

1. **Prepare the place.** A chair, and in front of it a table with a pillow on it at a convenient height, so that you can lean on it with your elbows.
2. **Sit** with the spine straight.
3. **Twelve Master breaths**: 7 seconds inhalation, 1 second stop, 7 seconds exhalation, 1 second stop, twelve times. The text gives the rhythm only and does not mention the colours of the First Master Arcane. The Note at the end recommends beginning with the Second Master Arcane instead, the standing and tensing exercise, facing the position of the Sun.
4. **Take the listening posture** (diagrams on page 68): elbows on the pillow, the head resting in the palms so that the eyes, forehead and upper cheeks lie on the palms and the four fingers of each hand; the thumbs, wetted with saliva, go into the openings of the ears to shut out all outside sound. See the note below on which fingers seal the ears.
5. **Relax completely** and close the eyes.
6. **Lift the eyes**: turn them as far upward as possible and converge them at the same time, "trying to see inside of your forehead between the eyebrows". The diagram calls this "looking on the mountain tops", toward the urna, "the central eye between the eyebrows".
7. **Forget everything** and concentrate your attention on the inner left ear.
8. **Listen through the sequence.** First the rumbling of the blood, which subsides; then "the shrill blast of a trumpet"; after a time the buzzing of a bee; next the ringing of a bell; when this is gone, the sound of a flute; then a lull; and then "the hum of the rhythm of the world", the sacred Aum.
9. **Return** to this state time and again and stay in it as long as you can or want. Find the true sound of Aum, and try to learn to sound it yourself.

## What the text says it develops

- Hearing "the vibration of the world, sound of rhythm which permeates the universe".
- Understanding: "Listen to it — you will understand."
- Learning "the sounding" of the true Aum.

## Phenomena and cautions

- The inner sounds in order: rumbling of the blood, trumpet blast, buzzing bee, ringing bell, flute, a lull, the hum of Aum.
- No warning is given for this Arcane.

## Notes for the reader

- Which fingers seal the ears is ambiguous. The sentence reads "with four fingers, of each hand, without thumbs, which you wet with saliva, and insert in the opening of the ears". Read strictly, the thumbs are inserted while the face rests on palms and fingers — the reading followed above, and the one that fits the posture. The small drawing on page 68, however, is described as showing the fingers over the ears. Either way the ears are closed.
- The order of posture and breaths is not fixed: the text describes the posture first and the twelve breaths after, followed by "then relaxed completely, close your eyes". The breaths may equally be taken sitting upright before leaning on the table.
- "Aum" here is the sound heard, spelled with "Au-"; the Ritual of the Pentagram and the Healing Power use the sung "Om".
- The glyph after "The Sacred Aum" on page 67 and the one after the Note on page 68 are in the manuscript's unidentified script and are not explained.
- The eye position is the same inward-and-upward gaze that opens the Concentration Arcane which follows.
