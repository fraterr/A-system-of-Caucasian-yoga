## In brief

The postures of the First and Second Master Arcanes carried into daily life. The Compendium gives them as "the sign of the Master System": how a practitioner stands when meeting another human being, and how he sits. Two captioned drawings later in the chapter name them the Sitting and Standing Master System Postures, "positive in relaxed attitude". Both are held in the Master breath.

## Technique

1. **Standing**, "when you meet another human being": stand straight, spine erect, shoulders back, chest forward, head up, chin in, gaze quiet and level with the eyes. Place the **right foot forward**, forming a slight angle (a V) with the left. Breathe the Master breath.
2. **Sitting**: sit erect, gaze level with the eyes, head up, chin in. Legs relaxed, with feet, shins and thighs forming the letter V. Master breath.
3. **Attitude**: "positive in relaxed attitude", so as "to receive, analyse and direct consciously impressions and expressions, according to the law and spirit of the times".
4. **Foot forward and breath.** Right foot forward "denotes Sun current (Ra), positive and electric; left foot forward would mean Moon (Ma), negative and magnetic". The sign uses the right foot; the Second Master Arcane chooses by the breath you are in.

## What the text says it develops

- It is the outward "sign" by which those in the Master System recognise one another, given alongside the pass-words Yat-Ha-Ah-Hu-Vai-Rio and Yat-Ha-Ah-Hu-Vo.
- The standing posture is "upright and on the level": "standing like a man in the Master System".

## Phenomena and cautions

None given.

## Notes for the reader

- The sitting posture is the seat of the First Master Arcane (hands on the thighs, V's of the feet and legs), and the standing posture is the first posture of the Second, minus the arm and colour work. The cards for those Arcanes give the full detail.
- The two passages come from different places in the Compendium (manuscript pages 48 and 52). The text at the foot of page 52 breaks off after "negative and magnetic"; the next surviving page begins the Lesser Arcanes.
