## In brief

The Seventh Master Arcane stands apart from the other six. The author says it "is a separate one called the Seventh Arcane, but having in itself a separate place as a purely magical work, connected with weather control". You stand at an altar with your hands under water in a wide vessel, facing the Sun, Moon or stars, and take twelve breaths in three kinds: exhaled through the mouth as a sigh, as a moan mixed with whistling, and as the roar of wind mixed with whistling. Woven into each exhalation is the word **I-Hau-Haa**, which the text says "awakens the elemental spirits of the wind, storm, hurricane". Through the "attracting powers of wind and storm" the Arcane "changes surrounding climatic conditions, with help of the mighty spirits El Borach (spirit of the lightning) and Waat (spirit of the wind)".

## Technique

1. **Set up** a stand or altar with a vessel on it: square, round, "pentagonal or other form", about two feet across and six inches high, half filled with pure water. The stand should be high enough to put the hands on top of it without bending or straining.
2. **Stand** erect two feet from the altar, **facing** the Sun, the Moon or the stars, "accordingly".
3. **Cleanse the lungs** by panting, throwing out all residual air.
4. **Submerge the hands** in the water, palms resting on the bottom, fingers spread fan-like, the thumbs and forefingers of both hands touching each other under the water (see the drawing of the hands).
5. **Eyes** fixed, "but very lightly, so that at times one is actually seeing only blur".
6. **Sighing breath.** Inhale deeply for 7 seconds, hold 1 second, exhale slowly through the mouth sounding a sigh until the lungs are empty; "voice should sound like deep sigh". Stop 1 second.
7. **Moaning breath.** Inhale 7 seconds, stop 1 second, exhale through the mouth "with a moaning sound, like moaning, and at the same time whistling". Stop 1 second.
8. **Roaring breath.** Inhale 7 seconds, stop 1 second, exhale through the mouth "with a roaring sound, like roaring of the wind, mixed with whistling of it". Stop 1 second.
9. **Interweave the word I-Hau-Haa** with the exhalation of each of the three breaths; it "forms the background for them, giving them vibration".
10. **Repeat** the set of three four times: twelve breaths in all.

## Timing

Only the inhalations (7 s) and the stops (1 s) are timed. The author says explicitly that the exhalations "are timed only to make them run naturally and easily, without prescribed 7 seconds". No total duration is given.

## What the text says it develops

- It "changes surrounding climatic conditions" through the "attracting powers of wind and storm", with the help of El Borach and Waat; the Index says it "brings in storm".
- Unlike the first six, the text claims no personal development for this Arcane: it is magical work, not training.

## Phenomena and cautions

No phenomena are described. The cautions are the light gaze (a blur is expected), the panting cleanse, and the untimed exhalation. The lines that follow call the seven Arcanes "the seven keys of absolute liberation" and the keeping of the Master rhythm "the short path".

## Notes for the reader

- It is filed as a Master Arcane because the manuscript numbers it VII, but the author sets it apart as "a purely magical work" and the Compendium counts the Great Work as "six Great Master Arcanes". Read it as a ritual rather than a daily exercise.
- One word in the sentence assigning I-Hau-Haa to the exhalations is blacked out in the manuscript ("to be interwoven with the exhalation of the air, in [word blacked out], sighing, moaning, and roaring"). The meaning is not affected.
- The Index spells the word "I-Hau-Ha"; the exercise text has "I-Hau-Haa". The card follows the exercise text.
- "Accordingly" for the direction to face is not explained; presumably one faces whichever luminary is in the sky, or the one matching the breath one is in, as elsewhere in the system.
- The word "blot" appears in the manuscript between "forefingers" and "of both hands"; it is unclear and has been left out of the step.
