## In brief

Between the dietary regimen and the kneeling exercise of the Twelfth Arcane the author places two short exercises for the throat, twice a day, five to ten minutes in all. Their aim, he says, is to "awaken and intensify" the thyroid and parathyroid glands so that their secretions strengthen and purify the blood and heighten the metabolism, dissolving and eliminating salts and waste.

## Technique

1. **Thumb under the chin.** Put the thumb firmly under the chin, the other fingers folded, and press slightly on the muscles there.
2. **Roll the tongue** backwards and forwards for 2½ to 5 minutes. You will feel the muscles ripple under the chin where the thumb rests; follow the ripple with the thumb, pressing slightly "to excite the contraction of the muscles". This is the first exercise.
3. **Chin to chest.** Bend the head down until the chin touches the chest.
4. **Tense** the muscles of chin and neck by stretching the mouth to both sides and downward, until all the muscles and tendons stand out on the neck.
5. **Lift** the head and chin high up without releasing the tension, "pulling them and stretching vigorously".
6. **Relax** neck and face, bend the head down again, tense, and repeat for 2½ to 5 minutes. This is the second exercise.

## What the text says it develops

- Awakens, purifies and excites the thyroid gland, which sends into the blood "secretions which are rejuvenating to the tissues and the body".
- Strengthens and purifies the blood and heightens the metabolism, helping to dissolve and eliminate salts and waste products.

## Phenomena and cautions

- At the beginning you will have pains in the throat, neck and thyroid area, "which is perfectly to be expected", since the muscles are not used to gymnastics; after a few days they stop. Start at 2½ minutes each and build up to 5.

## Notes for the reader

- The exercises follow directly on the juice-and-milk regimen; the author treats the two together as the preparation of the blood for the arcane proper.
