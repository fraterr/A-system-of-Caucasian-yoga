## In brief

The first of the eye exercises attached to the Seventh Arcane. You accustom the eyes gradually to the Sun, then immediately watch the image it leaves on the retina against a dark space or behind closed lids, holding it steady and drawing it toward you. The text says the image becomes "like a mirror from burnished glass and metal" in which you will see your own face and other objects, and that this helps to develop the clairvoyant faculty.

## Technique

1. **Gaze at the Sun** — before it crosses the meridian, and also mornings and evenings, at sunrise and sunset. Begin with a short time and lengthen it as the habit establishes itself.
2. **Turn at once** to a dark space or wall, or close the eyes, and watch the spot "photographed on your retina", for the same time you spent looking at the Sun.
3. **Hold it steady** and work to bring it nearer to you, to between six feet and one foot.
4. **Watch the mirror.** The image, the text says, becomes like a mirror of burnished glass and metal in which you will see the reflection of your face and different objects and things.
5. **Use also the Moon**, the planets and the stars, again before they cross the meridian.
6. **Indoors**, a hundred-watt blue electric bulb at three feet is given as a help in the development.

## What the text says it develops

- The faculty named just before it in the arcane: the clairvoyant state, seeing "things, objects, persons, and happenings and events".
- The steady after-image that becomes a mirror.

## Phenomena and cautions

- The retinal spot, first unsteady, then held and drawn near; the author's stated limits are the meridian rule and gradual lengthening.

## Notes for the reader

- The meridian rule for the Sun and Moon is given more fully in the doctrine on the eyes, the Sun, the Moon and the spot, and in the First Master Arcane.
- The next eye exercises in the chapter are the eye muscle exercises and splitting the ether.
