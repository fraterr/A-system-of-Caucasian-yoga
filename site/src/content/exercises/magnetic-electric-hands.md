## In brief

The exercise "for electrifying and magnetising of the hands", so that they "pour out the healing current" used in laying on of hands. It runs on the Master rhythm of the First Arcane (7 s in, 1 s hold, 7 s out, 1 s hold; twelve breaths; 3 min 12 s), each half-breath filled with seven circular rubbings of one hand by the other.

## Technique

1. **Stand or sit**, back straight, head up, chin in; a margin note says to face the Sun's direction. Exhale thoroughly.
2. **Inhale 7 s**, rubbing the left palm. Left hand palm up, right hand palm down on it; rub the left palm and fingers with the right palm in circles from right to left, seven circles in the seven seconds.
3. **Brush off, hold 1 s.** At the seventh circle glide the right hand away from you off the left palm, as if brushing something off. During the hold turn the left hand over, back up.
4. **Exhale 7 s**, rubbing the back of the left hand with the right palm, seven circles from right to left, and glide away again with the brushing motion.
5. **Hold 1 s**, turning the right hand palm up.
6. **Inhale 7 s**, rubbing the right palm with the left palm, circles from left to right, and complete the breath as before on the back of the right hand.
7. **Switch** back to the left and alternate until twelve breaths are done: six per hand, 3 min 12 s.

## What the text says it develops

- Electrifies and magnetises the hands so that they "pour out the healing current" to "alleviate pain" and stir up "the restorative processes in the human body".

## Notes for the reader

- The circles run "from right to left" when the right hand rubs and "from left to right" when the left rubs; from whose point of view is not said.
- "Rubbing each hand six times in varying succession" most naturally means simple alternation.
- Two words are illegible in the brushing-off sentence; the sense is not affected.
