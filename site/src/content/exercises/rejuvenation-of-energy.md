## In brief

XIII L. is less a single exercise than a theory with a set of practices attached. The text says the body's "battery of life energy" is concentrated in the spinal fluid; where the fluid touches the nervous centres it supplies them with the power of life. When energy runs low the organism stirs the fluid by itself, through yawning. The arcane teaches you to do on purpose what a yawn does by reflex. The author says it is "for increasing span of life" and is to be practised in important cases.

## The theory

- Yawning presses on the medulla oblongata, which raises the pressure in the cavities of the head and in the central canal (fourth ventricle) of the spine.
- A satisfying yawn is felt as a refreshing pressure inside the head spreading toward the ears, with a rumbling and a ringing "like with delicate silver chains", and ends with a deep inhalation felt in the solar plexus.
- The diagram on page 102 shows the ventricles seen from above with the central canal, and a section of the brain with the fourth ventricle, pons and oblongata marked.
- Two exercises besides yawning "redistribute the spinal fluid"; a further one, the tensing of the medulla, is said to develop the "pineal gland (medulla oblongata)" itself. The author uses the two names for one organ.

## Technique

Each practice has its own card; the manuscript gives them in this order and says the last cannot be understood without the earlier ones.

1. **Yawn** deliberately and study the pressure, sounds and deep inhalation that come with it (yawning-exercise).
2. **Jaw**: tense the chewing muscles without clenching; open the mouth wide with the eyes pressed shut; combine both with a hearty inhalation; twelve times or more each (jaw-exercise).
3. **Nape**: joined hands, bent fingertips at the junction of head and neck, head moved chin-to-chest and back twelve times or more (nape-exercise).
4. **Serpent**: inhale, lock the throat, raise a tension from the lower bowel to the throat and hold the pressure at the back of the head; learn to tense and relax the medulla at will (order-of-the-serpent).

All are to be used "when the circumstances call for it" and "according to the need for the expansion of energies".

## What the text says it develops

- A fresh supply of life energy to the nervous centres, and so a longer span of life.
- Relaxation of the nervous system, with the ability to tense again "according to the new pattern for thought".
- Growth of the medulla oblongata / pineal gland, "the one most important key to energy and power", and "straight path in selfrealisation and mastery".

## Phenomena and cautions

- Sounds in the ears (silver chains, rumbling, a hissing at the base of the skull), tears, and satisfaction in the solar plexus are the signs that the practices are done correctly.
- Pressure inside the head is the mark of tension; always relax the inside of the head afterwards.
- If the head starts to vibrate sideways, energy has overflowed onto the ether: stop and relax.

## Not a practice: the spinal-fluid transfusion

After the exercises the author adds that "in very important and rare cases, when life is at stake", when the energy in the spinal fluid is low because the fluid lacks youthfulness and quantity, "a operation can be performed" to replenish it. He describes it in one paragraph and no more: the assistance of "a trustworthy initiated pupil or Dashtur" is required; the donor is a young, healthy person of the same blood, "lovingly willing" to help; spinal fluid is drawn by puncture between the vertebrae from both people, a little from the recipient "only to establish the contact", more from the donor while "paying strictest attention not to injure in any way the donor"; the two fluids are mixed and injected into the recipient near the puncture. This card records that the text says this. It is not presented here as something to do, and the author himself fences it with those restrictions.
