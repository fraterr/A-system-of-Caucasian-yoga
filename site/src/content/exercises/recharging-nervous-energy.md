## In brief

The Ninth Lesser Arcane is less an exercise than an instrument. The author describes "a way used in ancient Egypt" of strengthening the currents of energy in the body: two rods, or "grips", held one in each hand while standing or sitting in the Master System postures. He says the Egyptian figures holding rods show exactly this, using the Second Master Arcane. The Sun rod is hardened coal, the Moon rod lodestone; together, the text claims, they release "secondary electricity" into the body, storing it in the "unipolar ganglias, and spinal fluid" and raising its potential one hundred percent for twenty-four hours.

## Technique

1. **Make or obtain the Sun rod.** Hard coal "specially hardened" — the author compares the process to converting iron into a magnet ("magness") by heating to high temperature and instantly quenching in water. He says the carbon rods prepared for electric arc lamps "are exelent as Sun rods of power". Six inches long, one inch in diameter, or sized to the grip of the hand. It may be inserted in a copper tube, ends open or closed.
2. **Make or obtain the Moon rod.** Hard or pressed lodestone, or a rod of magnetised hard iron or steel; same length and diameter. It may be inserted in a zinc or tin tube, ends open or closed. The Moon rod, the text says, "works as a kind of catalyst" to bring the Sun rod into more powerful action.
3. **Take a rod in each hand.** The text says "one generally to be used in the right, another in the left hand. One having the power of the Sun, another of the Moon" — read in parallel, the Sun rod in the right hand and the Moon rod in the left, which matches the system's pairing of Sun with the right side (this pairing is the card's reading; the text does not spell it out).
4. **Take the posture**: standing, as in the Second Master Arcane, or seated on a block as in the Egyptian figure. Be relaxed and follow the direction of the Sun.
5. **Do the Second Master Arcane** with the rods in the hands. (Filling in from that card: the standing, tensing exercise of twelve Master breaths, 7 s in, 1 s hold, 7 s out, 1 s hold, three each on red, yellow, blue and white, 3 min 12 s, the forward foot chosen by the breath.) The text gives no other duration for holding the rods.

## What the text says it develops

- Energy "akin to electricity (secondary electricity)" released into the body and stored in the unipolar ganglia and the spinal fluid.
- The body's energy potential raised "one hundred percent", lasting "a day and a night, (24) twenty four hours".

## Phenomena and cautions

- The text names no sensations; its one instruction is to be relaxed and follow the direction of the Sun.
- The author says rods of power "were known and used in the most remote even times", and that the secrets of their preparation were known to few initiates.

## Notes for the reader

- **Other materials.** The author lists alloys and minerals he says carry power: the "mysterious metal (bronze) Auricallum", five metals of definite colour (white, black, red, blue, yellow) in even proportion, said to be radioactive and to hide "the mystery of immaculate conception"; "electron" (40 % gold, 60 % silver); silver 75 % with zinc 25 %; copper with zinc; copper with tin. He gives the planetary metals — Sun gold, Moon silver, Saturn lead, Jupiter tin, Mars iron, Venus copper, Mercury mercury — as a key for other bronzes, and names coal, lodestone, beryl, amber, tourmaline, rock crystal, hematite. Bronze rods "can be used with II Second M. Arcane".
- **Symbolic objects** used the same way elsewhere: Babylonian "mares" (probably maces) of iron with silver inlay, with a horned human head or a bull's head, and the Tibetan dorgee.
- The card reads "magness" as magnet; the text does not explain the hardening of coal beyond the analogy with iron.
- The postures are those of the Master System (see the auxiliary card on the Master System postures).
