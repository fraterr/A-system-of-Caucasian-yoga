## The rule of the eyes

> The eyes are to be relaxed as to muscular structure and must not be fully charged with power from within. In Master exercises the eyes must play [hang] on the Sun, Moon: before they cross the meridian, star light, or a spot.
>
> Spot used as exercize point must be on white background and can be for general purposes black on which the charging colors should be imagined mentally. Size have to be about like 1 cent.
> <cite>ms. p. 13</cite>

The First Arcane places the spot "on the level with the eyes and about from three (3) to seven feet away on a vertical wall or stand"; the Third adds a second spot "three (3) to four (4) feet distant on the Earth (flat on it) or on the floor"; the Sixth says to "shift eyes around the spots' circumference, size of a penny", "without straining or staring"; the Seventh: "Eyes shold be fixed, but very lightly, so that at times one is actually seeing only blurr."

## Why the eyes

"Eyes absorb or emanate among others the N or Love rays, and that with they are the index of the soul — sending out love, will and thought." The First Arcane is said to develop sight and clairvoyance; the Seventh Lesser Arcane builds a whole training of the eyes on this.

## Sun shine, Moon shine, Star shine

"The light or shine, radiation and emanation or reflection of each of heavenly bodies is called — Sun shine, Moon shine, Star shine etc." Sun and Moon are used only "before they cross the meridian", i.e. while rising. In the Concentration arcane the practitioner is to "gradually get accustomed to gaze in the Sun… also mornings and evenings, at sunrise and sunset, begin with short time", then use the after-image; the Moon, planets and stars likewise, and "a hundred watt blue electric bulb at 3 (three feet distance)" as a substitute.

## Facing the Sun

> When concentrating, meditating receving or sending energy, thougt Master always faces direction in which Sun is situated at given moment. East in the morning, South at noon, West at sunset, and North at midnight. Unless there is a special need of Moon power for astral, or specific powers of different planets or stars — according to their magical properties.
> <cite>ms. p. 14</cite>

The reason given is that "Hertz waves are thought waves of the geoid entity the Earth Soul — Armaiti — whose aura we blend with our own in the sixth (6th) Master Arcane", and that these waves "follow the Sun" (Index). The instruction "face the direction of the Sun" recurs in most of the Lesser Arcanes; the Creation Exercise widens it: "face Sun, Moon, planets or stars according to things you work for". The two exceptions are the Ritual of the Pentagram and the Magical Projection, which face North.

## Position of the eyes in inner work

For the inner sound and for concentration the eyes are closed and turned "as far upwards as possible, converging… trying to see inside of your forehead between the eyebrows". The author calls this "looking on the mountain tops. Toward the urna. Central eye between the eyebrows." The Creation Exercise uses "eyes half close, introspective gaze", and notes that in the subconscious state "your eyes get out of focus… This is normal".
