## The scheme

> Developement of human entity is fourfold:— 1) Physical 2) Mental 3) Spiritual 4) Psychic.
> Colors corresponding to those functions are:— 1) Red. For the physical. 2) Yellow — for the mental. 3) Blue — for the spiritual. 4) White — for the psychic.
> <cite>ms. p. 12</cite>

The Compendium repeats it with the pigment names (vermilion, chrome, ultramarine) and a note that white "is mixture of red-yellow-blue" and that "in certain cases the colour for psychic is black". The Introduction adds "[for white race]" to the psychic line, and the Index promises a "diferentiation in the developement in different races" that the text never elaborates.

## Where each colour is fixed

| Plane | Colour | Regions charged (Introduction, ms. p. 12) | Regions charged (Compendium, ms. p. 44) | Inhalation expands |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Physical | red | lower stomach, sex, back of the head | lower part of the stomach, back of the head | abdomen |
| Mental | yellow | upper chest, forehead | chest, forehead | chest only |
| Spiritual | blue | solar plexus (abdomen), top of the head | upper abdomen (solar plexus), top of the head | chest and upper abdomen |
| Psychic | white | arms, hands, legs, feet, face | legs, forelegs, feet, arms, forearms, hands, face | chest, diaphragm and abdomen |

The last column is from the First Master Arcane, where the colour is also tied to a way of breathing: red fills the belly, yellow the chest alone, blue chest and upper belly, white the whole trunk. The Introduction glosses mental as "[intellect]" and spiritual as "[vitic energy]", the Compendium as "(intellectual)" and "(dynamic)".

## The order and the count

Always red, then yellow, then blue, then white: "three breaths for every developement", twelve in all. The Fourth Arcane doubles this to four a colour. The Fifth Arcane distributes the three breaths of each colour over two arm positions ("two with hands in front and one with hands on the sides"). The Sixth and Seventh Arcanes drop the colour sequence entirely.

## Two places at once

> Filling with energies is done simultaneusly in two places in the body, both of them being chared at the same time during the period of exhalation.
> <cite>ms. p. 44</cite>

This is the constant rule: inhale the colour, exhale while "by thought and willing" fixing it in the pair of regions. The Introduction calls this "basic part of Master Exercize".

## The planes elsewhere in the book

The four planes are the axes along which everything is measured: slavery exists "on one or more planes"; the Fourth Lesser Arcane is for tiredness "physically, mentally, spiritually or psychically"; the Light of illumination "is to be seen on every plane — physical, mental, spiritual and psychic". The sleeping postures assign the physical and spiritual planes to the Sun breath and the intellectual and psychic to the Moon breath.
