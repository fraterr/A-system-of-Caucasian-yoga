## The aim

Walewski states the purpose of the whole book in one sentence of the Compendium:

> In the work on Mastery the main and the only object is to attain communion with the Harmonious Creative Power of the World, and consciously joining the forces for solving the destinies of the evolution toward Immortal Oneness.
> <cite>ms. p. 40</cite>

"To master", he explains, "means to overpower, to subdue to rule, to know; to understand thoroughly, — it means also dominion: superiority, victory". The first step is "mastering thought and thru it mastering the body". The process of development is called "the Great Work", and it consists of the Master Arcanes, "constituting the 'Short Path', quintessence of the ways and means, for developement of conscious mastery". (The Compendium says six arcanes; the end of the Seventh Arcane says seven, "the seven keys of absolute liberation".)

## Slavery and its causes

The starting condition is slavery, "produced by ignorance, on one or more planes of his entity, i.e. physical — mental — spiritual and psychic". Its sources are listed in order:

- the "preconceiving and conceving state of the mother", the prenatal influence of her thought, feeling and willing (the Mother's Breath at conception, see [Breath is life](/doctrine/breath-is-life/));
- "the moment and manner of birth", compared to "taking a fish out of water into the air", when "first impressions mould the destiny, by shaping and piersing new channels for energies and life currents" (with the odd practical note that the umbilical cord "should be cut only after it collapses 3 times");
- childhood, puberty and adolescence, "of seven years each", when adverse influences "warp its growth".

Liberation "could be only formulated and be given out by the souls who attained the fredom… It was done by the Masters, and is called the Master System, from eternity into eternity for the guiding of human race."

## What the system claims for itself

The claims are total. The Master System "proves that everything is one, built from the same clay of primordial energy"; it "is solving every problem, in every departament of life, is ansvering every question, and meets every situation — on the physical, — mental, spiritual and psychic planes"; other systems "have been borrowing from it" and fall "below the standart of first and the only word of Masters of Arias". It is "always given only for the sufficiently developed and ready canditates… Those only hear the call who are ready".

## What the Short Path does

After the Seventh Arcane the author sums up what the seven exercises are for:

> This short path establishes the master rhytm in the body which you must strive to keep as often as you can… This will put you with the good thought, good word and good deed, and open channels, establishing connections with the Master Thought, and Heavenly Father. You have then all the knowledge, all the authority, and all the powers and forces at your command. You are on the path and your Great Teacher — the Heavenly Father will instruct you in everything personally, so that you do not need any other teachers, or human authorities.
> <cite>ms. pp. 39–40</cite>

The practical content of that sentence is the [Master rhythm](/doctrine/master-rhythm/): the whole method is to breathe 7 · 1 · 7 · 1, with the four colours, as often as possible, in the seven postures.

## Reading note

The book's opening line, "It was said 2000 years ago, that there is nothing hidd that would not be revealed", and its closing pages on illumination frame the exercises as a revelation of something previously secret. The author names his sources only in passing: the Zend Avesta and Ahura Mazda, the "Master System of Egypt" seen in the postures of statues, and "Hindoo" terms (Kumbhaka, Kundalini) used as parallels.
