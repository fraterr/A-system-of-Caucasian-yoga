## The breath as indicator

The Introduction sets up a small psychology: the entity is "I — I am — soul", with three attributes, "I think; I feel; I will". Then: "Indicator of thinking, feeling and willing is the breath." Everything that follows in the book is a way of acting on the person through the breath.

## The Mother's Breath

> A being is born to this world having as basic rhytm, — breath of the mother at time of the conception this is called the Mother's Breath and within its vibration are hidden the destiny and fate of every individual.
> <cite>ms. p. 9</cite>

The Master rhythm is meant to replace this inherited rhythm: to be receptive to the flow of Gaya-Lhama "is to establish master rhytm in the human being and relingon the mothers impression of adverse soroundings, coincidences and influences at the time of conception".

## The three breaths

> Flowing thru the nostrils, breath shades itself three ways.
>
> When coming thru the right nostril it is creative electric breath and it is feeding the vasomotor system. So called Pingala. — Name of this breath is **Sun Breath** {breath of warrior ready to fight}.
>
> Coming thru the left nostril, it is regulating and mothering principle — magnetic — it is feeding the sympathetic nervous system so called — Ida — name of this breath is — **Moon Breath** {breath of the sage ready to absorb wisdom}.
>
> Coming, evenly thru both nostrils it is balancing = preserwing or destroying. It is called **Shushumna Breath**.
> <cite>ms. p. 10</cite>

"Normally the breath is changing about every hour — sun breath — neutral (Shushumna breath and moon breath." The First Master Arcane adds the qualities: Sun, right nostril, "heating and electrical"; Moon, left nostril, "cooling and magnetic". In the standing postures the Sun current is "Ra" and the Moon current "Ma".

## Why it matters in practice

- **Every Master Arcane begins with "note whether in Sun or Moon breath".** The general note in the Third Arcane asks for an even number of practices in each breath: if the morning session was in Sun breath, make the next one in Moon breath.
- **The side that leads.** In the Second Arcane the foot put forward follows the breath ("in Sun breath right foot, in Moon — the left"); in the Fourth the arm that rotates first is "the side in which breath is flowing".
- **Sleep.** To gain energy on the physical and spiritual planes, fall asleep on the left side (which establishes Sun breath); for study and "peacefull endeavors", on the right (Moon breath). "Try always to balance the number of positive and passive attitudes."
- **Tuning others.** The Fifteenth Arcane reads a person's state from the depth and rhythm of their breath and joins it.
- **Conception.** The Sixteenth Arcane makes the sex of a child depend on which breath each parent is in.

## Changing the breath at will

Two postures "create sun or moon breath in 3 (three) minutes": lying on the left side with the head on the left hand for the Sun breath, and the same reversed for the Moon breath. They are given as a separate card: [Sun and Moon breath posture](/exercises/sun-and-moon-breath-posture/).
