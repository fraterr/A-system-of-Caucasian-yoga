## The sign

> The sign of Master System is, when you meet another human being, stand straight, (erect) spine straight, shoulders back, chest forward, head up, chin in, gase quiet, level with the eyes. Right foot forward, forming a slight angle with the left. (V). (Master Breath)
>
> When you sit, sit erect, gase level with the eyes.) Head up chin in. Legs relaxed feet, forelegs and tighs forming letter V. (Master Breath).
> <cite>ms. p. 48</cite>

The Index calls these the "signs and pass word. Postures of recognition". The plates two pages later name them the Sitting and Standing Master System Posture: sitting is "positive in relaxed attitude, to receive, analise and direct consciously, impressions and expressions"; standing is "upright and on the level… Right foot forward denotes Sun current (Ra) positive and electric, [left foot forward would mean Moon — (Ma) — negative and magnetic." The forward foot, in other words, declares which breath one is in.

The sign is the everyday form of the seated posture of the First Arcane and the standing posture of the Second. Both are annotated "(Master Breath)": the posture is to be held in the 7 · 1 · 7 · 1 rhythm. The "pass word" is never spelled out; the Fourteenth Arcane names "determination" as "the password".

## The Egyptian argument

The author repeatedly reads Egyptian statuary as evidence for the system:

- The seated First Arcane posture "is the same in Egyptian ritual — only in exoteric doctrine it was shown with limbs brought togeter, without showing the state of relaxation partaining to esoteric doctrine".
- The standing Second Arcane posture, one foot forward: "[The same posture is in Egyptian ritual]".
- The Fourth Arcane profile with the hand raised over the eyes: "Note! Profile posture can be recognised in Egyptian system".
- The rods of power: "It was shown in the figures, using the Second Master Arcane exercise. Two rods clasped in the hands of standing figures", drawn as two Egyptian figures, standing and seated.
- The bird "clasped to the back of the skull" in the Sixth Arcane is "the Ka or bird (Ba) of the Egyptian mythology"; the locked throat is "represented as Crux Ansata in Egypt"; the winged scarab "representing human skull".

## Other postures of the body in the book

- **Kneeling** at a chair or two staffs (Sixth Arcane); kneeling upright with the arms overhead and **prostrating** with forehead and forearms on the ground (Mullah Mudra, Twelfth Arcane), "This is called prostrating oneself".
- **Lying** on the left or right side to change the breath; on the back on grass or sand to gaze at the sky; sleeping with the head toward North.
- **The arms floating** "as if about to fly" (Fifth Arcane).
- **Head in the hands** with thumbs in the ears (Soundless Sound, dream work, crystal gazing).
