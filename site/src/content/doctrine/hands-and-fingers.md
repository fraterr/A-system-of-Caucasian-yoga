## The meaning of the fingers

> Thumb is logic and will power, index finger is destiny (command), middle finger is teachers (used in drawing on the sand, and for explaining), third finger is humanitarian love, compassion, and altruism, little finger is sex, lust, physical love.
> <cite>ms. p. 97</cite>

Practical rules follow: "Do not stick out the little finger, it means over sexuality. Never fold the thumb under the other fingers when making a fist, it denotes weak will, poor health, and propensity for lying." Fingers pressed together "emphasis the attributes ascribed to them". Developing the thumbs "grows and strenghtens the will power".

The scheme is applied in the Doctrine of the Heart, where the index and middle fingers ("destiny and teacher") are pointed at the heart, in the "sacerdotal hand"; and in the silent form of Demand-Command, where the thumbs press "on knukles of the fingers having desired attributes".

## Right gives, left receives

> Giving and conveying hand is the right hand, and it should be put on the suffering part of the body, while the left hand should be put on the opposite side from pain, as a receiving pole for the healing power.
> <cite>ms. p. 99</cite>

The Healing arcane says the same. Rods of power are Sun in one hand, Moon in the other. The Ritual of the Pentagram is drawn with the right hand, index finger pointing; the magical circle is drawn with the chalk in the right hand "while the fist of the left is tightly closed with the thumb covering and pressing the index and middle fingers".

## The V of the hands

In the seated Master Arcanes the hands rest on the thighs "with fingers slightly separates forming V's… Life attracts life, symbol V has concentrating power and sounds V and (F) and (PH) have direct mantric invoking) power to attract, increase and prolong." The feet, shins and thighs also form V's, and in the standing posture of recognition the right foot forward forms "a slight angle with the left. (V)".

## Hand positions in the exercises

- Seventh Master Arcane: hands under water, "fingers spread fan like, thumbs and forefingers of both hands touching each other".
- Creation Exercise: hands over the solar plexus, fingers "pressing deep into the stomack at the sternum part".
- Fourth Master Arcane and Demand-Command: the clenched fist, tensed, striking the earth or the desk.
- Splitting the ether: hands clasped with forefingers upright, "the thumbs of both hands are tuching each other".
- Concentration and dream work: face resting on the palms, thumbs (wet with saliva) in the ears.
- Fifth Arcane: "hands limp from the wrist", then folded on the breast.

## The currents of the hands

Shaking the relaxed hands "with so quick motion as to blurr the vision" is said to leave "the strong vibration in your hands, comparable to the electric current"; the [magnetic-electric exercise](/exercises/magnetic-electric-hands/) charges them in the Master rhythm for the [laying on of hands](/exercises/laying-on-of-hands/). Pressing the fingertips and the muscle under the thumb "bring[s] harmony into body processes".
