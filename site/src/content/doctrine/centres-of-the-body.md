## A note on the author's anatomy

Walewski writes "pineal gland (medulla oblongata)" and "medulla oblongata or pineal gland" repeatedly, as if the two were one organ; he also sets "pituitiary is epiphysis" beside "pineal gland or hypophysis", reversing the usual names. The glossary keeps his usage. What he means by the "medulla" is functionally clear: the centre at the base of the skull where the breath's energy is released, where tension is felt, and which "can be tensed at will".

## The solar plexus

The seat of desire. In the Creation Exercise the hands are placed "over solar plexus, on the part where is ensiform cartilage" and the diaphragm is shaken "until you will feel the hunger and pang of the desire in your solar plexus, often also felt like heat"; the jolt of locking the throat then sends "spiritual energy of longing and desire thru the spinal column upwards to the brain". In Demand-Command: "Feel your desire in your solar plexus", and pounding the fist "shakes the solar plexus, transmuting the image of the desire into the head, where it is willified". The third of the ancient initiations, by air, "affected the breath and solar plexus". Yawning fills it: "a deep satisfactory inhalation felt as pleasantly filling and pervading the solar plexus".

## The medulla and the path up the spine

The Creation Exercise describes the ascent: heat and pressure "mounting within the spine, striking pineal gland (medulla oblongata), where it is felt like a snap, then reaching forward, beetwen the eye broows, with another snap, and finally the top of the head". The Sixth Master Arcane lists what the current passes through: medulla oblongata, corpora quadrigemina, fourth and third ventricles, corpus callosum, corpora striata, pons Varolii, pituitary and pineal, septum lucidum. The Fourth Lesser Arcane "is sending currents to pineal gland (medulla oblongata) stimulating it. This is the serpent within the spinal column, raising his head in attention". The hands-at-the-nape exercise "has a great influence on medulla oblongata or pineal gland, developing its size and capacity".

## Ganglia and spinal fluid as batteries

The Fourth Master Arcane "is developing the commanding will — and electricity storing it in ganglias of the body ready to use". The rods of power release energy "to be stored in unipolar ganglias, and spinal fluid. Raising the potential of energy one hundred percent, and lasting for a day and a night". The Thirteenth Arcane makes the fluid the reservoir: "Body battery of life energy is concentrated in the spinal fluid, and the fluid tuching the centers supplies them with the power of life"; yawning "presses on medulla oblongata… augment pressure on the cavities in the head, and the central canal".

## The urna and the three tension points

Between the eyebrows is the "Urna", the "central eye", toward which the closed eyes are turned. Concentration is felt as "a slight pressure or tension" there. The Order of the Serpent teaches to tense, separately and at will, three points: "at the base of the skull — (the back of the head) then in the front of the head — back of the point between the eyebrows, then on the top of the head", and even the right and left sides of the head. The author's summary: "the soul as a eternal I, I am… is revolving beteween the three points in the head". Thought, he says, "is formed (electrically) in magnetically tensed formation in the brain, which magnetical tension can be relaxed of tensed thru the work of medulla oblongata."
