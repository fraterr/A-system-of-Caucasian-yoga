## The triad

> Good thought, good word and good deed, are analogous with **Master thought Master word and Master deed** expressions with the aspects of Ahu-Ra-Maz-Da. [Light]
>
> Bad thought, bad word and bad deed, are analogoos with **slave thought, slave word and slave deed**, expressions with the aspects of Angro-Mainous. (angry-mind) [Darkness]
> <cite>ms. p. 47</cite>

"Good thought, good word, good deed" (humata, hukhta, hvarshta) is the ethical formula of the Zend Avesta, which the author names as his authority: "Books of Zend Avesta, declare the Mastery and ansver the ridle of human life:— why I am here?" The answer is the epigraph of the whole book, repeated in the Compendium: "I am on this earth — to reclaim the earth, — to turn the deserts into paradise, — a paradise most suitable to God and his associates. To dwell there in."

## What "Master thought" means in practice

The passage in the general points on the Master Arcanes is the most concrete:

> Master thought is the opposite of slave thought as is Master will and feeling, and we must mster our thought, our will, and our feelings — by relaxation in positive attitude; relaxed body but alert and watchful thougt, will and feeling, so we attain to radio-audio reception and transmission at one and the same time. As Masters we are the transmitter and the receiver! The eclesis, synthesis and analysis: — the filler, thinker and willer.
> <cite>ms. p. 14</cite>

The same attitude is named twice as the "state of mastership": "to be at all times consciusly in positive receptive, attitude, open to all positive powers to flow and express thru you. And to direct them in the proper channels which will be illuminated and express good thought, good word, and good will." The sitting posture of recognition is described in the same terms: "positive in relaxed attitude, to receive, analise and direct consciously, impressions and expressions".

## Impressions and expressions

The pair "impression / expression" is the author's shorthand for the whole traffic between world and self. Slavery comes from "mislead impressions on the human system"; the Master System "thru the conscious control of the breath, and establishing the Master rhytm… we change our imressions and expressions — from slavery into Mastery". The mirror image is his:

> We are mysterious mirror, which in its pure state reflects the world, its cases and effects. If then inside of you, you creat the cause, effect will follow = and be reflected thru the mirror of the soul — into any medium becoming flesh… Always strike at the causes, changing them, and the effects will follow.
> <cite>ms. p. 48</cite>

## Connection with the Master Thought

The Master rhythm, the author says, "will put you with the good thought, good word and good deed, and open channels, establishing connections with the Master Thought, and Heavenly Father." In the Fifteenth Arcane the same breath "tune[s] yourself and others on the Master thought, and rhytm of the Geoic entity". "Master Thought" with capitals is thus something one connects to, not only an attitude: the thought of the Earth-soul (Armaiti) and of the "Heavenly Father", who thereafter "will instruct you in everything personally".
