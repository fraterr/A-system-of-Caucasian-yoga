## The two great words

The book opens (Key to Mastery) and closes (Magical Projection) on the same formula:

> ∴ Yat-Ha-Ahu-Vairio ∴ The will of the Lord is the law of righteusness ∴
> <cite>ms. p. 1</cite>

This is the *Yathā Ahū Vairyō*, the chief prayer of the Zoroastrian Avesta, which the author writes in several spellings (Yat-Ha-Ah-Hu-Vai-Rio, Yat-Ha-Ahu-Vairio) and in a script of his own resembling Avestan. He calls it "Master Protection". It is blown over wounds in the Healing arcane, sung by crowds to tune them to the Master rhythm, and inscribed around the magical circle.

The second word is a shortened, forceful form:

> "Yat-Ha-Ah-Hu-Vo-" the will of the Lord is power (lightning that strikes
> <cite>ms. p. 48</cite>

It is the word of the Ritual of the Pentagram, one syllable per stroke: Yat — Ha — Ah — Hu — Vo, then "Om" on the spiral and thrust. "It is the word that rooster knows the word 'Om' is the word tha lion roars." In the Magical Projection it means "The will of the Lord is might."

## Aum / Om

"The Holy and Sacred 'Aum'", "the hum of the rhytm of the world", is what the Sixth Lesser Arcane teaches one to hear with the ears stopped; the practitioner is to "find the true sound of 'Aum' and try to learn the sounding of it". As "Om" it closes the pentagram word and the healing word, "vibrating the 'Om' to the end of the breath", and it is the sound for tuning a group "for a period of five to fifteen minutes". In the Sixteenth Arcane the final sound of the man's exhalation, "Om", "conweys the spark of life".

## Sounds without words

"Sounds V and (F) and (PH) have direct mantric invoking power to attract, increase and prolong" (First Arcane). The Seventh Arcane uses sighing, moaning and roaring; the Sixteenth notes the gasp, hiss and sigh of the sexual act; yawning "sends out tremendously powerful vawes on the eather". Slow "long sonorous mantrams" calm; quick ones "tense, harden the brain, bring in hysterical state" (Fifteenth Arcane).

## Names of powers and spirits

Ahu-Ra-Maz-Da (Light) and Angro-Mainous ("angry-mind", Darkness); Armaiti, "the Earth Soul"; El Borach, "spirit of the lightning", and Waat, "spirit of the wind"; the Heavenly Father and the Guardian Angel; Ra (Sun current) and Ma (Moon current). The Compendium's word for the ultimate is Ga-Ya-Lha-Ma, "the That Is".
