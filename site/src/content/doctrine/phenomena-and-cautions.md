## Signs that an exercise is "working"

The author repeatedly gives a sensation as proof that the exercise is done correctly.

- **Sound of silver chains** in the ears when the jaw is tensed (Exciting the life energy; the first exercise of the Thirteenth Arcane: "The sound proves that the exercise is done correctly"); a "rumbling sound" and tears when the mouth is opened wide and the eyes pressed shut; the ringing "like with delicate silver chains" in yawning.
- **Hissing** at the base of the skull after the nape exercise: "result of percolating of the spinal fluid, to and from the fourth ventricle".
- **Heat and pressure** mounting in the spine, a **snap** at the medulla, a second snap between the brows, then the top of the head (Creation Exercise).
- **Pressure between the eyebrows**, "at times… slight pinching", in concentration; breathing may stop by itself, "this should be so".
- **Tingling** of face and body after five minutes of strong breathing (Twelfth Arcane), "showing strong oxydisation of the blood".
- **Congestion in the heart**, "pressure of feeling and fullness in the cardiac region" (Doctrine of the Heart).
- **Pains in the throat and neck** in the first days of the thyroid exercises, "perfectly to be expected".
- **The sequence of inner sounds**: rumbling of the blood, blast of a trumpet, buzzing of a bee, ringing of a bell, a flute, then "the hum of the rhytm of the world" (Soundless Sound).

## The phenomena of the Sixth Master Arcane

The fullest description:

> You will feel heat waves and electric shocks at the base of the skull, and in the cerebellum and inter-brain and magnetic current flow along the spine upward into the medulla oblongata… you will hear a pulsating sound like a bell or chime and feel pulsations with a sense of swelling or expansion of aura, and sometimes a feeling as if beating or folding of wings, of moving as if a bird was clasped to the back of the skull or head, — this is the Ka or bird (Ba) of the Egyptian mythology.

## When to stop

- **Darkness.** "When things suddenly go dark, you stop, or if you continue, remember that you will go into a sleep or trance state, and must not be disturbed until your Guardian Angel or Heavenly Father awakens you" (Sixth Arcane). The trance "howewer is healing and gives the power of laying on of hands".
- **Levitation.** "If your knees rise from the floor or body rises in the air, stop at once. — You do not want levitation to occur" (Sixth Arcane).
- **The head shaking.** "If during the exercise you find that your head begins to vibrate sideways, — (shaking motion) it means that the energy is pent up to the point of overflowing… then you must stop the exercise and relax" (Order of the Serpent). After every such exercise, "relax inside of your head completely, by mowing your head on the neck".
- **Eyes out of focus** in the Creation Exercise are "normal and to be expected"; "working those powers is best beginning delicately, and being in practice with Major Master Arcanes".

## Restrictions on use

- The Ritual of the Pentagram as a weapon: "use this only when you know that it is the only way, to act in accordance with the Spirit of the Law".
- Mental connection at a distance: "This exercise is not to be played with, and not for curiosity sake, do it to help."
- Demand-Command: "It is not necessary of ever using this arcane in front of others. You should do it alone."
- The tongue stick: "should not be done to a pregnant woman, as it would bring about miscarriage".
- Magical Projection: the circle "is protection from your own volitional and intentional emanations, which grow to the extent of being very dangerous"; "be ready for rebound".
- The spinal-fluid operation of the Thirteenth Arcane: "only in very important and rare cases, when life is at stake", with "strictest attention not to injure in any way the donor".
- Kechara Mudra and the rectal tube (Mullah Mudra) are given with practical care instructions (salt on the wound; keep the tube clean; expel the air before removing it).

Unfinished or missing cautions: the Index promises a "Caution as to surroundings!" for the Sixth Arcane and a "note" for the Seventh that are not in the surviving pages.
