## The passage

> In the ancient mysteries, establishing of the Master Breath and Master rhytm, was done by the four great initiations of the elements.
>
> 1) First initiation was by water. Submersion in cold water, affecting thyroid, brought about a spasm, which when correctly done established the Master vibration in the body. This was initiation of Moses and Christ, and used in this day in Christian Church.
>
> 2) The second initiation was by fire. The neophite passing between two fires, or going thru fire, had to hold his breath, for the impossibility to inhale the smoke. (Babylonian and Dravidian mysteries).
>
> 3) The third initiation was by air, dropping down from a height in specially prepared contrivances. This process affected the breath and solar plexus. (Egyptian mysteries, also Christ put by Satan on the mountain and then thrown from it).
>
> 4) The fourth initiation was by earth, getting body covered with earth, being burried alive for certain period of time, also listening to the (silence) in subterenian caves, to get the sacred rhytm of the earts heart, which vibrates in unison and harmony with the univerce. (Heart of the earth cotracts for seven second, one second pause, seven seconds expansion one second stop) this is the sacred Master rhytm.)
>
> Those are the greath four initiations in the mysteries of Mastery.
> <cite>ms. pp. 49–50</cite>

## How the author reads them

Each initiation is explained as a physiological shock that resets the breath: cold water on the thyroid, enforced breath-holding, the gasp of a fall, the silence of burial. The Master System's claim is to obtain the same result by "the simplified, condensed and correct — manner": the exercises. Several of them reproduce the elements in miniature. Holding the breath for seven seconds in the Sixth Arcane is the fire initiation's breath-hold; the cold and hot water on the feet in the note to the Second Arcane ("cold water is used for magnetic shock and hot for electric") is a small water initiation; the thyroid exercises of the Twelfth Arcane work on the gland the water initiation "affected"; and the earth initiation is the origin the author gives for the [Master rhythm](/doctrine/master-rhythm/) itself.

## Magnetic and electric

The water and earth initiations are, in the author's vocabulary, magnetic; fire and air electric. The same polarity organises the book: Moon breath and Sun breath, lodestone rod and coal rod, "wading in rivers… dewy grass or sea is magnetic. Walcking on dusty roads, sand, earth is electric", the magnetic pole and the electric Sun of the Magical Projection.
