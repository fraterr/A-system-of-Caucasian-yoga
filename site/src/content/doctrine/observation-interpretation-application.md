## The method

> In this key is given simplified, condensed, and correct manner how to master, how to solve every problem, answer every question, in every departament of life — in physical, mental, spiritual and psychic planes.
>
> It is the key to meet every situation by applying the consciusly developed 1) careful observation 2) correct interpretation & 3) practical aplication.
>
> To know thyself — is to go inside of ones entity and to watch study and apply.
> <cite>ms. p. 8</cite>

The three steps are invoked again for the dreamer who becomes conscious in sleep ("use principles of — careful observation, correct interpretation and practical application") and for the Masters who think "trough the other egos… trough its wisdom of careful observation correct interpretation and practical application". In the Concentration arcane, holding a thought at the point between the eyebrows and turning it over "on all the modes and manners" is what "is called careful observation".

## The definitions

The Compendium turns the three steps into a classification of the occult disciplines:

> (1) Mysticism is careful observation thru supersensitive channels of impressions.
> (2) Occultism is correct interpretation, thru applying of the conscious discrimination of the soul.
> (3) Magick is practical application of supersensitive observation and interpretation. It is to make things appear, disappear and change one thing into the other.— Creation, destruction and transmutation.
> <cite>ms. pp. 51–52</cite>

By this scheme the Master Arcanes and the eye exercises are mysticism (training the channels of impression), the doctrine is occultism (interpretation), and the Lesser Arcanes of creation, projection, command and the Magical Projection are magick.

## The entity: I, I am; I think, I feel, I will

The Introduction sets out the self in a diagram:

> Indicator of entity is — I — I am — soul. I think; I feel; I will; } attributes. Indicator of thinking, feeling and willing is the breath.
> <cite>ms. p. 8</cite>

The formula returns wherever the practitioner has to assert himself: in the Fourteenth Arcane ("concentrate on the self realisation. — I, — I am — ponder on the truth of your being, consider the attributes of your ego — I think, I feel, I — will"), in the Magical Projection ("Confirm the 'I' and 'I am'"), and throughout the Illumination chapter, where "'I, I am' is the majest of dignity". The three attributes are also the three powers by which Gaya-Lhama is "appropriated": "loving of… thinking of and willing it".
