## The definition

> A Master breath is 7 seconds inhalation — 7 sec. exhalation and 1 second stop or hold at each end (16). The Master rhytm is 7 seconds. This correspond to the rhytm of the heart center (esoteric) of the Earth — + (plus) (with the pause of 1 second) (+).
> <cite>ms. p. 13</cite>

The First Master Arcane spells out the arithmetic: one inhalation of 7 seconds, one pause of 1, one exhalation of 7, one pause of 1 — "sum one breath sixteen — 16 seconds". Three breaths on each of the four colours make twelve breaths, 192 seconds, "or thre minutes and twelve seconds 3′ 12″. Which comprises a full 1st Master Exercize". The figure 3 min 12 s recurs through the book as the standard length of a Master Exercise, and is also the rest to be taken kneeling after the Sixth Arcane.

## Where the rhythm comes from

The fourth of the ancient initiations, by earth, was "listening to the (silence) in subterenian caves, to get the sacred rhytm of the earts heart, which vibrates in unison and harmony with the univerce. (Heart of the earth cotracts for seven second, one second pause, seven seconds expansion one second stop) this is the sacred Master rhytm." The same idea returns in the Fifteenth Arcane: "the highest rhytm is Master Rhytm… By this breath you tune yourself and others on the Master thought, and rhytm of the Geoic entity."

## What it is for

At the close of the Seven Arcanes: "This short path establishes the master rhytm in the body which you must strive to keep as often as you can." The rhythm is not only a timing for exercises; the instruction is to live in it. The [standing and sitting postures of recognition](/exercises/master-system-postures/) are both annotated "(Master Breath)".

## Two practical details the text gives

- Before any Master Arcane, "first exhale all breath" (the sentence continues on the missing manuscript page 19; the Fourth and Fifth Arcanes say to "exhale all residual air from the lungs using muscles of the abdominal diaphragmatic and chest sections").
- The pauses are counted, but the exhalations in the Seventh Arcane "are timed only to make them run naturaly and easily without prescribed 7 seconds".
