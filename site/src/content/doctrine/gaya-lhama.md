## Two descriptions

The Introduction speaks of it as a power seeking a channel:

> Titanic power — Gaya-Lhama — is everywhere, and always seeking entrance into human being to express herself thru it to be receptive to the harmonius flow of this power — is to establish master rhytm in the human being…
> <cite>ms. p. 9</cite>

and, a page later, as an energy with structure: "Gaya-Lhama — which is energy contained in the space, has 4 (four) states of vibration which correspond to 4 (four) colors and which being assimilated from the air. Have centers in the human body and vivfy them."

The Compendium gives the metaphysical version. Matter is "condenced energy — positive and negative in different proportions"; positive and negative are sparks divided out of a neutral vacuum; and

> This one which is also two, which is also two with again one sorrounding, which is three — is the "That Is" — and is called the — ∴ **Ga-Ya-Lha-Ma** ∴.
> <cite>ms. p. 43</cite>

## How it enters the body

The author's physiology is specific. Air enters the nose and is "given spiral motion by turbinates, and elongating into two cones striking each other as they meet"; it is heated in passing, and in the pharynx "releases the Ga-Ya-Lha-Ma which sinks thru the pharynx behind the soft palate in the proximity of twelveth nerve and first pair of cervical nerves, in the proximity of medulla oblongata, ninth, tenth, and eleventh nerves. Air then goes to the lungs where it oxydises the blood." The energy, that is, parts company with the air at the back of the throat and goes to the [medulla oblongata](/doctrine/centres-of-the-body/), which the author treats as the master centre.

## The four states and where they are stored

"Filling with energies is done simultaneusly in two places in the body, both of them being chared at the same time during the period of exhalation." The four states, their colours and their places are set out under [The four planes and colours](/doctrine/four-planes-and-colours/). The point to hold on to is the mechanism: the colour is drawn in on the inhalation and *fixed* in the two regions "by thought and willing" on the exhalation.

## How it is appropriated

In the First Arcane: "Keep your thought fixed on Gaya-Lhama — [Ga-El-Lha-Mah] the centralizing principle of life, which is only appropriated by loving of (and so attracting) thinking of and willing it. This act by those three powers fixes this energy in the physical, intellectual, spiritual and psychic equation." Loving, thinking and willing are the three attributes of the "I" from the Introduction; the exercise is meant to apply all three at once.

The Third Arcane gives the affirmation that goes with it: "I am breathing life in on my breath — and forming a field of magnetic force around me, my magnetic field or aura".

## Spelling

The word is written Gaya-Lhama, Gayalhama, Ga-Ya-Lha-Ma and, as a pronunciation guide, Ga-El-Lha-Mah. The author gives no etymology.
