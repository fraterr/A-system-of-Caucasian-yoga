## The Light

Before the conclusion, illumination is first described as an experience in the Concentration arcane:

> During the meditation and contemplation always look for the light, and it will come to you, so bright, that the light of the Sun will seem only a shadow in comparison with it. It is real, it is to be seen on every plane — physical, mental, spiritual and psychic. This is illumination, "the Light".
> <cite>ms. p. 68</cite>

## The centre of the universe

The chapter headed "Illumination. (Conclusion)" begins:

> You are the mathematical and geometrical center of all the universe, where the centre is you, and radius goes into the infinity. Instead of going after things command them to come to you. You are the Lord in your universe which is **the** universe. Desire, wish and will, order, demand command.
> <cite>ms. p. 117</cite>

"The moment you realise and become fully conscious that you are the centre of the universe, you are that centre." The image for the connection with everything is the spider "in the centre of the webb feling and receiving the impressions from everything everywhere and adjusting the equilibrium of forces". The riddle of "Know thyself" is answered with "God is immortal man — man is mortal God", and motion itself is reinterpreted: "You do not move, when you walk or ride, the soroundings move according to the laws of the equilibrium". "This is magick, this is the miracle. I am that I am."

## The stages of the ego

The author then describes the growth of the "I" as a physical expansion:

1. **The spark.** "Originally the 'I' the ego is small within the body a iota, a spark", strengthened "trough the exercises of receiving the impressions and wrestling with them to transmute them into the expressions". The body is outside it; the world is far away.
2. **Saturation: the Saint.** The ego "grows and reaches the bound prescribed by the limits of the flesh… a perfect fit, it is unity, the seed grown to the size of its container… 'Eat of the bread it is flesh of mine, drink of the vine it is blood of mine.' A stage of the Saint."
3. **Expansion: Masters and Saviours.** "The ego overlaps the bounds of the flesh, ego becomes the outside, while the body becomes the seed on the inside, this is immortality, the way of the Gods." Such an ego "merges other smaller egos within its scope of attraction" ("the ego of leaders and executives") and is "bent on solving the karma of nations and races".
4. **Arhats.** Thinking, feeling and willing "go on far ahead of the body", "not within the head but above at 30° or 45° degrees", in abstract space; the body is "only as a root, a embryo — 'I am the vine, ye are the branches'". "This is the way of Arhats. 'I and my Father are one.'"

Two words in the description of the third stage are drawn as blank boxes in the manuscript ("it thinks [BLANK] then [BLANK] in (abstract) space"), apparently never filled in.

## Neighbours, friends, faithful friends

The chapter ends with a classification of the people such an ego meets, "according to their inner true self, which can not be hidd from the All Seeing Eye": neighbours, friends and faithful friends, the last of whom "burn the vigil light — to show the way".
