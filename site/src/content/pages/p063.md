Without stopping swing the arm in a wide circle, after escribing which continue making half a circle toward the center at the same time stepping forward with the right foot, and making a rhrust with the arm and hand, forefinger pointing. (Actually the circle and half a circle form a spiral drawn in the air from left to right.)

Note (all the exercise is done pointing the index finger as if writing in the air.)

[DIAGRAM: Eight step drawings of the pentagram being traced, each with an arrow showing the direction of the stroke. Labels: "First motion" (single upward stroke), "Second motion" (inverted V), "Third motion" (V with cross stroke), "Fourth motion", "Fifth motion" (closed pentagram), "Sixt motion" (pentagram inside a circle with a half-circle inside), "Complete." (pentagram in circle with inner spiral), "Complete with Zend mantram." (pentagram in circle with Avestan script characters written around the ring and inside the points). At lower right, two drawings of a hand, closed with index finger pointing upward, labelled "Right hand."]
