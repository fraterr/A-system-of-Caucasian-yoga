With every heart beats utter word Al-Im, this is the holy word which opens the heart. The word is repeatted with beats of the heart, and you enter the interior of the heart, filled with red clouds and mist, in the center is standing the arc, with measures of discrimination, and over the arc you see the blasing pentagram, with the all seing eye in the center.

You worship then and commune, and send out love, to understand, reach, help and bless.

Then having ended, you put the right hand like in the beginning of the Arcane and withdraw the left hand, and with index finger and middle on the heart repeat with every beat of the heart word "peace", to fill the heart with it.

[DIAGRAM: A heart containing a pentagram with an all-seeing eye in its centre. Labels: "Love" above, "Al" at left, "Im" at right, "Peace" below.]

This completes the Doctrine of the Sacred Heart.

Note. Heart shold be consulted in every important undertaking. By putting on it the two fingers of the left hand. It also is used in healing. ∴
