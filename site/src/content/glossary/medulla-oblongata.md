Gaya-Lhama is released from the breath "in the proximity of medulla oblongata"; the rising energy of the Creation Exercise strikes it "like a snap"; yawning presses on it; the nape exercise develops "its size and capacity"; the Order of the Serpent teaches to "tense your medulla at will". The author writes "pineal gland (medulla oblongata)" as one organ and swaps the names hypophysis and epiphysis; the glossary keeps his usage.
