Said to be "the rhytm of the heart center (esoteric) of the Earth", learnt in the ancient earth initiation by listening in caves. Twelve such breaths make 3 min 12 s, the standard length of a Master Exercise. The instruction after the Seventh Arcane is to keep this rhythm "as often as you can".
