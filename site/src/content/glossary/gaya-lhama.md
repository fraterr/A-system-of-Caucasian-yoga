Walewski's central term. In the Introduction it is "titanic power… everywhere, and always seeking entrance into human being"; in the Compendium it is "the 'That Is'", the one-which-is-also-two-which-is-also-three. It enters with the air through the nose, is given a spiral motion by the turbinates, is released in the pharynx near the medulla oblongata, and has four states of vibration that correspond to the four colours and the four planes. In every Master Arcane the thought is to be "fixed on Gaya-Lhama", which is "only appropriated by loving of, thinking of and willing it". The spelling varies through the manuscript; the phonetic gloss Ga-El-Lha-Mah is the author's.
