---
term: "Urna"
aliases: ["central eye", "third eye", "point between the eyebrows"]
short: "The point between the eyebrows toward which the closed eyes are turned in concentration and inner listening: \"the central eye\"."
related: ["medulla-oblongata"]
pages: [68, 69, 71, 107]
---
The eye position is "called looking on the mountain tops. Toward the urna." Concentration is felt there as "a slight pressure or tension". It is the second of the three tension points of the head, and the tip the perfected tongue should reach in Kechara Mudra.
