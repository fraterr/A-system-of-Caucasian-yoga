In the Creation Exercise the hands press into it "at the sternum part (ensiform cartilage)" until "the hunger and pang of the desire" is felt there; a jolt then sends the energy up the spine. Pounding the desk in Demand-Command "shakes the solar plexus, transmuting the image of the desire into the head". Yawning fills it with "a deep satisfactory inhalation".
