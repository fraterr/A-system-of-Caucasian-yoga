The Roman-numbered Lesser Arcanes carry the letters "L." (I, IV–VII, IX–XI, XIII–XVI), "G." (II, III, VIII) or "G.S." (XII) without explanation; the Index calls the group "Lesser Arcanes and great exercises". The Creation Exercise is called "a great Arcane (hermetic)"; the Compendium counts "6 (six) Great Master Arcanes" though seven are given.
