term: "Four planes"
aliases: ["fourfold development", "four states of vibration"]
short: "Physical (red), mental (yellow), spiritual (blue), psychic (white): the four functions of the human entity, the four states of Gaya-Lhama, and the four colours breathed in the Master Arcanes."
related: ["gaya-lhama", "master-exercise"]
pages: [15, 16, 46, 47]
---
Each colour is fixed on the exhalation in two regions of the body: lower belly and back of the head; chest and forehead; solar plexus and crown; limbs and face. Mental is glossed "intellect", spiritual "vitic energy" or "dynamic". In certain cases the psychic colour is black.
