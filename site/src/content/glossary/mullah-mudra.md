---
term: "Mullah Mudra"
aliases: ["Mullha Mudra", "breathing through the rectum"]
short: "Prostration with a small tube inserted in the rectum so that air passes in and out of the large intestine with the breath: for hibernation, rejuvenation and cleaning of the bowel."
related: ["kechara-mudra"]
pages: [9, 85, 86, 87, 90]
---
The posture without the tube is the Twelfth Arcane's rejuvenating exercise. Ten to thirty minutes; expel the air before removing the tube.
