---
term: "Kechara Mudra"
aliases: ["Khechari mudra"]
short: "\"Swallowing the tongue\": inserting the lengthened tongue past the soft palate into the nasal cavities, as preparation for hibernation and to \"center the life energies in the head\"."
related: ["mullah-mudra", "trance"]
pages: [9, 83, 84]
---
Prepared by daily pulling and "milking" the tongue with a cloth and loosening or cutting the frenulum; "real perfection is reached when one ca touch with the tongue, point between the eyebrows".
