the forefinger of the right hand is outsreched tip of it tuching the forefinger of the left hand, the thumbs of both hands are tuching each other.

Now separate the forefingers of both hands and make the distance between them same as the distance between the pupils of the both eyes. Hold the hands about two (2') feet from your eyes, and look at the forefingers until you will see in the center betwen them, the third finger, composite of two forefingers. (Having on its sides two fingernails.

Study this composite finger until you see it perfectly, and when becomes to you a absolute reality. Move your hands farther and nearer trying to keep the third finger image steady
