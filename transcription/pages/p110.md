## XIV L. Arcane. Demand, Command.

Striving to attain the things in life, realise your correlation to it, - sit down at the desk or table facing in the direction of the sun. Relax, and concentrate on the self realisation. - I, - I am - ponder on the truth of your being, consider the attributes of your ego - I think, I feel, I - will. Feel youaself being conscious of yourself, within the center of your brain receiving the impressions and transmuting them into the expressions. Have a clear conception and understanding of the words - "I can" - "I want" - "I must" "I will" concentrate on the object of your desire, imagine it clearly and plainly. - Feel your desire in your solar plexus. -

When the realisation of the inewitableness of your desire will dawn upon you - inwoke the password which is determination.

Inhale deeply and hold your breath locking it with pharynx. Lift your righ hand closing the fist. (The thumb covering the other fingers). Tense the fist and the arm.

Now suddenly and with power bring the arm and the closed tense fist down on the desk or table, at the moment of striking relaxing the fist and releasing the air from the lungs.
