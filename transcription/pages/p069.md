You will find that inside of your forehead, between the eyebrows you feel a slight pressure or tension, at times the feeling may be that of slight pinching. Hold to this feeling, not letting it relax, do not pay attention to your breathing or your body, (at times after exhalation or in the middle of it you will comfortably stop breathing, this should be so.)

Now take the thought on which you concentrate, try to so uise [squise?] it in the point between the eyebrows where you feel the pressure. Hold it with the pressure, repeated in front of it, and inside of it, on all the modes and manners, half it, split it, the thought and the words contained in it. You will know then, this is called careful observation.

(+) At certain times you will see light before your eyes, it may be a dot a star a eye, a vision of Heavenly Father, a Guardian Angel etc. To see those things pertains to second stage of concentration called when the subject is of spiritual importance appears – meditation.
