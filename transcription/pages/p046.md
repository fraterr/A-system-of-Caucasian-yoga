Filling with energies is done simultaneusly in two places in the body, both of them being chared at the same time during the period of exhalation.

The four states of vibration of Ga-Ya-Lha-Ma, and corresponding colours, with the parts to be chaged in the body are: -

1) **Physical** - colour red (vermillion), parts of the body to be charged - lower part of the stomach and back of the head

2) **Mental** (intellectual) - colour - yellow (chrome), parts of the body to be charged - chest and forehead.

3) **Spiritual** (dynamic) - colour - blue (ultramarine), parts of the body to be charged - upper part of the abdomen (solar plexus) and top of the head.

4) **Psychic** - colour white which is mixture of red-yellow-blue), parts of the body to be charged - legs, forelegs, feet and arms, forearms, hands, and face. (In certain cases the colour for psychic is black).
